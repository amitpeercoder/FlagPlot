### The order's paper: YOUR PLAN (rule 27)

11. **The part.** A fold, YOUR PLAN, after LIMITS and before ACTS, on the paper of a new order to the staff whose end is "seize and hold" an island, and on no other paper. Not in a watched game.
12. **Its lines are read from the staff's reading,** and from nothing else: one line for each entry of `preview`'s `with_change.tree.sub_intents` whose `kind` is `take_place` and whose `parent` is the order's number, in the order the reading of the bare order (no `under`) gives them. That is the staff's own list of the places the side does not hold; the page does not work out what the side holds. A line is named by its place, "The landing at Lunga", the place said as the page says any place.
13. **The words.** Closed, the fold's head is `YOUR PLAN · ` and then: `The staff plans every landing.` where nothing is written; `Yours: the landing at Tulagi, the landing at Lunga.` where something is, in the order sent; `The staff plans no landing under this order: there is none to write.` where the reading has a tree and no such line. Where the reading was refused, or carries no tree (the staff proposes nothing), the head is `YOUR PLAN` alone, the fold has no line, and open it says `There is no reading of this order to take its landings from.`: the page says nothing of who holds a place, since no tool told it. Open, a line not written says `left to the staff` and has the button `WRITE IT`; a line written has its own lines and the button `LEAVE IT TO THE STAFF`, which puts the whole landing back.
14. **A written landing's own lines,** each at the staff's choice until set, with the controls and the way back (BACK TO THE STAFF’S CHOICE) the LIMITS part gives a take today, and under the names it gives them there: `Ships and forces` (task forces only: no single ship is offered or sent), `Land units`, `Supplied from` (the forces limit's `source`: one limit has one name on the paper), `Not before`, `Wait for`, `After`, `Knowledge`, `Risk`, and `By the end of` (a day, the take's `by_day`). `Ships and forces` and `Land units` are "use only" lists: "none of" is not offered on a written landing and no `none_of` is sent. `Risk` offers the staff's choice, preserve, balanced and press: at the staff's choice nothing is sent and the landing reads its order's risk; any of the three that is set is sent, balanced among them, since under an order at press a landing at balanced is not the staff's choice. On a draft the way back is the page's own, for nothing has been sent. Air groups, an area, targets and protected ships are not offered on a written landing. `After` offers the other written landings of this draft, by name, and the other orders of his it offers today; it does not offer a landing that already waits, itself or through another, for this one.
15. **What is sent.** `preview` of the order, its `limits` as today, and `under`: one entry for each written landing, `{intent_kind: "take_place", hex, intent, by_day?, limits}`, with `limits` as the limits model gives them for these lines (forces, not_before, wait_for, after, knowledge, then risk where one is set, item 14). The entries stand in the order of the lines, but that a landing another waits for stands before it. Each carries its number: the order's number as the last reading gave it (`intent`), plus its place in `under` counted from 1; an `After` that names a landing of the draft is sent as that number. Where a reading comes back with another number for the order, the page asks again. The page sends no force, base, day or limit the player did not set.
16. **The model is pure,** beside the limits model and cut and run in node by the check as that is: `planLines(tree, order)` gives the lines of item 12, an empty list for a tree with no such line, and null where there is no tree; `writtenUnder(order, written)` gives `under`; `planSummary(lines, written)` gives the words of item 13 after `YOUR PLAN · `, and nothing where the lines are null. The names are fixed here because the check calls them.

### A reason that leads to his node (rule 31)

27. **A reason turns on a node of his** where, by numbers alone: its `serves.intent` is a node of his; or its `limits` list names one (a reason with no `limits` key is read as one with none); or its words carry the handle `intent:K` of one. A node of his is an intent with `maker` "commander" and a parent, from `command_state`, or a landing of the draft, from the reading's `under`. A handle, `kind:N`, in a reason's words is read as an identifier, as the page already reads one to say a name; no other word of a reason is matched, and the page does not read the words "the commander's" for it.
28. **Where it leads.** A handle of his node in a reason's words is said as `your landing at Lunga` and is a link. Once the number has said the node is his (item 27), the engine's words beside the handle that say whose it is are dropped, by a fixed list of readings (`writtenMarks`; R2 and `HIS_LANDING_READ` in the check): the link says it, and the page's own "the commander's" to "your" would otherwise leave "your landing at Lunga, your, is in force". A reason that turns on a node of his, shown anywhere but under that node's own line, ends its WHY with `YOUR PLAN` and a link for each such node, `The landing at Lunga`. In a plan's paper the link opens that plan's paper at his node's line; in the reading of a draft it opens YOUR PLAN at that landing. A limit of his node in a reason's list is under YOUR LIMITS as today (decision 0222, rule 17) and leads to that limit's line on his node's line.
