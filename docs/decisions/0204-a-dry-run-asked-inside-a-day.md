# 0204: A dry run asked inside a day

Status: a plan by the architect, written on Nov 21, 2026 on the designer's word at the table: a dry run refused at 0600 because "a dry run is asked at dusk" makes no sense to a commander, and it should be played from whatever is known at that hour. To be read by a reviewer who has not seen it written, and amended on that reading, before its hand calculation is written and before any of it is built.

Summary: A dry run (a trial, decisions 0175, 0178 and 0181) is refused while a day is open, at 0600 and at 1200. This lets it be asked there. It is played from the state as it stands at that hour, taken as if it were this evening: the first day played is tomorrow, when the order would in truth be taken up, and the hours of today still to come are not played. The paper says so. Playing the rest of today inside the dry run is left as a second stage.

Standing: Planned on Nov 21, 2026; waits for its review.

## Why

What is so today:

- **A seat may stop the clock at 0600 and 1200** where the scenario allows it (decision 0156). There it may still state an order to the staff, and that order is taken in at the next dusk: the staff is asked once a day, before the night.
- **A trial asked there is refused twice over.** The session refuses it by its own check, and under that the side's half of a picture is refused "while a day is open, since a picture is of an evening" (decision 0170, 3). Decisions 0175 and 0178 both list "a trial inside a day" as left for later.
- **A preview asked there is not refused.** The session already makes a copy of the game with the open day treated as closed (`as_if_day_closed`, decision 0156, as built), and works the preview on that: the state as it stands, with no pulse still to come resolved and no end of the day run.
- **Nothing flies between pulses.** Strikes in flight and sorties flown are empty outside the air steps of one pulse. What a side has part-way through a day is kept in its state: where a force is on its leg, its fuel, fatigue, groups stood down for the day.
- **A trial builds its own worlds** from a side's view and what it believes, starts each at dusk, and plays whole days. It never touches the game.

So the order a commander weighs at 0600 would be taken up this evening in any case, and the game can already show the staff the state as it stands at 0600 with the day treated as closed. Only the trial is shut out.

## The rules

1. **A trial may be asked while a day is open,** at 0600 and at 1200, by every form it has at dusk: of an order in force, of a draft, beside another, at later dates, and described.
2. **It is played on the copy the preview uses:** the game as it stands at that hour with the open day treated as closed. The trial is begun on the side's view of that copy, and everything the session reads to shape the trial (what is in force, the leaves held, the commands that stand) is read on the same copy, so the two cannot disagree.
3. **The first day played is the day after the open day,** and the commands of the course are handed in on its first morning. That is the dusk at which they would be taken in.
4. **The hours of today still to come are not played.** In the trial's worlds each force is where it is at the hour asked, with the fuel it has then. The pulses of today still to come, the fight ashore of today's end and today's end-of-day supply are not in the trial.
5. **Orders to units queued for the next pulse** are handed in with the orders queued for the coming day, as the preview does.
6. **Hits on men that wait for today's fight ashore are left out,** as a picture leaves them out.
7. **A contact's age is read as the picture holds it,** in pulses, and counted in hours as if the pulse last resolved were an afternoon. A contact of the night just past is therefore taken to be up to six hours fresher than it is.
8. **The answer says how it was asked:** the day and the hour, and that the rest of that day was not played. The staff's words of the trial say it in one sentence. The table shows it in the head of the paper: "From the morning of day 2, as things stand at 0600 of day 1", and under the costs: "The rest of today is not played."
9. **The game is as it was.** The copy is never resolved and never hashed; the trial's worlds are its own.
10. **The refusal goes.** Its words leave the session, the agent's instructions and the table.

## Stage 2

11. **Playing the rest of today.** Each world of the trial would start with the day open at the hour asked, resolve the pulses still to come with no staff asked, close the day, and go on. It needs a picture that is not of an evening: a day in hand, the hits on men that wait, and ages counted from the pulse last resolved. That changes what a picture is (decision 0170) and is planned on its own, if games show that the hours left out matter.

## Rule card

Reads: the game's state as it stands inside a day, through the side's view of a copy. Writes: nothing in the game. Draws no random number in the game; the trial's own draws are as they are. Adds no state.

## Games on file

None moves. No game on file asks a trial inside a day: it is refused today. The agent interface's version rises, since an answer gains a field and a refusal is no longer given.

## What it does not do

- It does not play the hours of today still to come (stage 2).
- It does not ask the staff inside a day. An order to the staff given at 0600 is still taken in at dusk (decision 0156, 6).
- It does not change what a picture is: the half is still taken of a closed day, here a day treated as closed.

## What it costs

- **A copy of the game for each trial asked inside a day,** as a preview costs today.
- **An answer that is a little wrong in a known way:** forces start some hours short of where they will be this evening, and whatever today's remaining pulses would have cost or changed is not in it. The paper says so.
- **Decision 0156's line that the copy is for the staff step and the check of a command, "nothing else",** is widened to the view a trial is begun on. The copy is still never resolved further.
- **Rulebook:** chapter 8 where it speaks of a trial and of what may be done at 0600 and 1200; the glossary's "trial"; the agent's instructions for stopping inside a day.

## Questions for the designer

None. The designer asked for this at the table; the choice to take the state as it stands "as if it were this evening" first, and to leave playing the rest of today for a second stage, is the architect's, on the size of the two pieces of work.
