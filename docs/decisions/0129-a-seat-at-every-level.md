# 0129: A seat at every level: a level's staff may be a person, a model or the game's own staff

Status: the designer's direction of Nov 16, 2026, with his answers to the three questions it raised, put to him one at a time by the architect with a recommendation on each. It adds to decision 0119 and to `docs/design/command-levels.md`. Nothing is built. It shapes the plan of stage 2b, which is written and confirmed before that stage is built.

Summary: Each level of command on a side is a seat that a player may fill, a player being a person or a model, and one player may fill several; the game's own staff fills every seat no one takes. A seat's reports are written by the game and the occupant may add a note; a model in a seat hears nothing but what reaches it through the game, and for people talk outside the game is a house rule; and an intent goes down with its reason

Standing: Decided by the designer, Nov 16, 2026. Adds to decision 0119. Not built

## The direction

The designer's, in his words as the architect took them down: each level can be played by a different player, a player being a human or an AI agent; a player may take the role of one level of a side, or of several, and acts as the staff of that level.

It follows from decision 0119. Once each level has its own picture and a fixed contract with the levels above and below it (intents and what bears on them come down; orders go to its own units; narrower intents go down; reports go up), nothing in the contract says who does the thinking.

## What is decided

1. **A level's staff is a seat with one interface.** What a seat is given: its level's picture, the intents addressed to it with what bears on them and their reasons, and its own forces. What it hands in: intents for the levels below it, orders for the units in its own hands, and, where it gives them, reasons.
2. **The game's own staff is the default occupant** of every seat, and stays deterministic code, so that the game runs offline and replays exactly, as the pillars have it. What a person or a model hands in from a seat is recorded as a player's commands and orders are today, and a replay plays the record.
3. **A player may fill one seat or several** of one side. The seat of the top commander is the one the pillars' player has always had.
4. **Relieving a commander** (decision 0119, on refusal) is replacing the occupant of a seat. The game's staff takes a seat that is left.
5. **An intent handed down takes effect at the level below on the following day,** and every seat of a side plans at once on what it held that morning. This settles who goes first in a day with several seats on a side, and is the delay decision 0119's clock already gives.

## The three answers

6. **The game writes a seat's reports, and the occupant may add a note.** Sightings, claims, losses, position and fuel go up as the game records them, with the delay and loss of any report. The occupant cannot alter or withhold a fact. A note is free text carried with the report, and is marked as the occupant's word.
7. **A model in a seat hears only through the game:** its level's picture, and the notes that reach it with reports and intents. So the fog between levels holds in every test that uses a model. People cannot be kept from talking, so for them talk outside the game is a house rule, stated at the start of a game and recorded in it.
8. **An intent goes down with its reason:** what it serves one level up and what would change it. Not the superior's whole picture and not his other intents. This adds to answer 3 of decision 0119, which sends down what bears on the intent.

All three are as the architect recommended.

## What follows

- **A reason is asked of every seat, and required only of the game's staff.** A person may give one or not. A superior cannot ask why of a seat that gave none, which is a cost to the second pillar that the designer accepts for a seat he has handed to a person.
- **A test the plan did not have:** a model in a subordinate seat, given only what a seat is given. If it cannot act sensibly on an intent, the intent handed down is not enough to act on. Stage 2b's proof uses one such seat under a superior played by the game's staff.
- **Stage 2b's plan is written to this interface,** so that the game's staff, a model and a person all fill a seat the same way.
- **Scope.** The pillars cut multiplayer infrastructure, and this stays inside that cut: seats are filled through the tools and files that exist, with no lobby and no server. Several people on one side waits until after the kill gate.
