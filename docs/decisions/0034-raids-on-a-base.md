# 0034: Raids on a base

Status: confirmed by the architect, within the plan for scenario 6 the designer approved on Nov 11, 2026. Decisions 3 and 6 are choices the plan did not state and are marked for review.

## The gap

Scenario 6 is four weeks of raids on an airfield. Until now a strike could only be sent at a contact, which is a sighting of ships (decision 0026). A base cannot be sighted, cannot be attacked, has no guns, and nothing at it can be hit.

Stage 6a adds the raid itself. Damaged aircraft and repair, operational losses, fatigue, pilots and the supply a base uses come in stages 6b to 6d.

## Decisions

### The order

1. **An air group can be given the mission "base strike" with a base as its target.** The order names the base. A group on this mission does not strike ships.
2. **Both sides know where every base is and which side holds it.** That is the map, not intelligence. Neither side knows anything else about an enemy base: not its aircraft, its supply or its guns.
3. **A base strike launches in the morning pulse, once a day** *(needs review)*. It launches if it is not dark where the group is, the group can fly at least one aircraft, its type carries a weapon, and the base is within the type's strike radius. It needs no contact and reads no belief. A raid on a fixed target is planned the day before and flown once; a strike on ships answers a sighting and may fly in either daylight pulse.

### The raid

4. **A base strike is a raid like any other** (decisions 0027, 0030 and 0033). The strikes of one pulse from one place at one hex are one raid, whether they are sent at ships or at a base. Escorts go with it if the base is within their radius. The defender's patrol over the hex is divided among all the raids attacking that side there. Each raid's escorts and its share of the patrol fight as before.
5. **The base's guns fire at a raid on the base.** A base has a number of anti-aircraft shots, as a ship has, and they fire as a force's do. Ships in the hex do not fire at a raid on the base, and the base does not fire at a raid on the ships.
6. **Each bomber left drops at the bomb's chance to hit, whatever weapon its type carries against ships** *(needs review)*. A torpedo bomber sent at an airfield carries bombs. Each shot is rolled on its own.

### What a hit does

7. **Each hit lands on aircraft on the ground, on supply, or on nothing,** with chances given in the air combat figures. One draw for each hit.
8. **A hit on aircraft destroys one aircraft on the ground.** The aircraft on the ground at a base are the ready aircraft of the groups there that are resting, and of the groups there on a strike mission that did not launch in this pulse. Groups searching or on combat air patrol are in the air. They are counted at the start of the air combat step, and taken from the lowest-numbered group first. A hit on aircraft when none are on the ground does nothing.
9. **A hit on supply destroys a fixed weight of the base's supply,** given in the figures, and no more than the base has.
10. **The size of the bomb does not matter yet.** One hit is one aircraft or one fixed weight of supply.
11. **The raiders learn nothing.** No report follows a raid on a base, because a side holds no belief about a base's contents. All the hits of all the raids on a base are worked out on the state at the start of the step and then applied, as for ships.

## Why

- **One mechanism.** The raid, the escort, the patrol, the guns and the per-shot roll are the ones scenario 5 built. Only the target and what a hit does are new.
- **No runway damage.** An airfield shut by bombing is a second state with its own repair rule. Four weeks of raids wear a base down through its aircraft and its supply, and those are states the base already needs. Runway damage waits for something that needs it, such as a bombardment.
- **Once a day.** Rabaul to Lunga Point and back is over 1,100 miles. One raid a day is what a group can fly at that distance, and at short range the cost of flying more will come from fatigue in stage 6c. Until then one a day keeps a nearby base from being raided twice as often as history allows.

## What it costs

- A bomber's load does not matter against a base. A type that carries two tons counts as one that carries a quarter of one.
- A base with no aircraft on the ground wastes the hits that land on aircraft. A defender can keep aircraft safe by keeping them in the air, and until fatigue arrives that is free.
- The attacker has no damage assessment, so nothing yet tells the staff a base has been beaten down.

## Deferred

- Bomb load by type, and aircraft damaged but not destroyed (stage 6b).
- Runway and facility damage.
- A report of what a raid saw.
- A second raid a day at short range.
- Night raids.

## The legibility test

A cheap model was given the Allied briefing and the log of the first two days of the scenario's own seed. It explained why the patrol fired fewer shots at the bombers on the second day, and why only the resting group lost aircraft on the ground. It predicted the third morning exactly: 15 bombers, 5 escorts, and the shots and expected hits of all four rolls. It saw that the patrol is worn down until every fighter is held by an escort, and that the resting group is safe only off the ground.

It found one gap. The attack event gave the hits on aircraft and on supply and left the reader to work out the rest. The event now says how many landed on nothing.

| Reads | The group's mission and target base; the map's bases; aircraft able to fly; the type's strike radius; the patrol over the hex; the base's anti-aircraft shots; the missions and ready aircraft of the groups at the base; the base's supply |
| Writes | Ready aircraft of groups on the ground; the base's supply; aircraft lost in the air, as decision 0027 |
| Events | Base strike launched; base attacked (shots, chance, expected, hits, and where they landed); aircraft destroyed on the ground; base supply destroyed |
| Randomness | The attack: one draw for each bomb, then one for each hit, keyed on the air group and the base. The base's guns: keyed on the base and the raid's first group |
