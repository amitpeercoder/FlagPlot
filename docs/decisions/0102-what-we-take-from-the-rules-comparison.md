Summary: What we take from the rules comparison (`docs/rules-comparison.md`): each gap it names is ruled build, defer or decline, by whether it changes which choices are sensible in the first 39 days of Guadalcanal. Build first: an airfield damaged and mended (0103) and a beaten side falling back (0104); then refuelling that costs time, claims that can fall short, and submarines

Standing: The order approved by the designer, Nov 14, 2026. Adds no rule

## The test

The comparison ends with it: a missing mechanism needs attention when it changes which choices are sensible, makes a constraint of the record disappear, or lets the planner exploit a simplification. Each gap is put to that test against the target of step 3, the first 39 days of Guadalcanal. Matching the other game is not a reason.

## Build, in this order

| Gap | Why it passes the test | When | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| An airfield cannot be closed by bombs or shells | Closing the field was the purpose of the raids and shellings of the period. Today the holder of the airfield flies whatever lands on it | Before the slice is lengthened | 0103 |
| Men of a beaten side stay in the hex | The garrison of 7 August left the field. Today it stays, unfed, among the men who beat it | Before the slice is lengthened | 0104 |
| Refuelling costs no time and no berth | A staff that plans by trial will find that fuel is free at a base and plan on it | Before stage 3 of step 3, where the staff issues orders | To be written |
| A claim is never below the truth | A claim of nothing is certain knowledge, and a planner can lean on it | Decided with stage 4 of step 3, the trial | To be written |
| Submarines | A carrier was torpedoed inside the period, and the threat shaped how carriers were handled. The research is in (`docs/research/oob/outside-research-nov-14.md`) | After stage 5 of step 3, before the campaign runs past 14 September | To be written |

## Defer, each with what would bring it back

| Gap | Brought back by |
| --- | --- |
| Range, manoeuvre and ships that do not get to fire in a surface fight | Reopening the misses at Savo Island and Cape Esperance that the designer accepted at the close of step 2 (`docs/validation/step2-close.md`). Not before step 3 is done |
| Weather | Research rows on days the weather stopped flying in the period, and a measure the lack of it makes miss |
| Fire that spreads and flooding that goes on | A measure of ships lost or saved that misses for want of it |
| Aircraft that come home damaged from a fight, and return fire from bombers | The air measures, when they are next tuned |
| Pilots one by one, and what an aircraft type is good at in a fight | Experience for air groups, which waits on research (decision 0064) |
| A port damaged; a port's daily ceiling; ships too large for a port | The rule on refuelling above, which may need the second |
| Rearming in port, and wear from steaming | A campaign long enough to need them: the stretch target of step 3 |
| Reports that are late or never arrive; reconnaissance of a base; evidence weighed across reports | The intelligence design (`docs/design/intelligence.md`), which step 3 leaves to a later step |
| Terrain in the fight ashore; men who recover; replacements for land units; supply moved overland | The tuning of the ground figures on the two attacks of the period, if it cannot be done without them |
| Mines | A scenario whose record has them |

## Decline

| Gap | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Industry, resources, research and shipbuilding | The game is an operation, not a war economy (`docs/pillars.md`). `docs/design/economy.md` keeps what is thought about materials |
| Political points, and the cost of moving a headquarters | No such choice is the player's in this game |
| Ship upgrades and conversions | A ship is what its facts say on the date of the scenario |
| Squads, guns and vehicles one by one; where a hit lands and what it pierces | The game counts men and sizes ships by toughness on purpose. Nothing in validation has asked for more |
| Rules of one theatre or one period | Outside the South Pacific in 1942 |

## What does not change

`docs/step3-spec.md` stands. The two rules built first are ground rules, as decisions 0095 to 0099 were, and sit before the lengthened slice in the same place. The rule on refuelling is added to what stage 3 builds on when its record is written, by the change procedure the spec gives.
