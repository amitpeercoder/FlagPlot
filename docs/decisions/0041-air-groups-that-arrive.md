# 0041: Aircraft that arrive on a day

Status: confirmed by the architect, on the designer's standing word of Nov 11, 2026 to decide rule details inside an approved plan.

Summary: Aircraft that arrive on a day: a scenario may deliver aircraft and pilots to an air group on a named day, raising its strength

Standing: Confirmed

## The gap

Replacements (decision 0038) top a group up to the strength it started with, by a steady daily number. Henderson Field was not reinforced that way. Whole squadrons flew in on known days: 19 fighters and 12 dive bombers on 20 August, 11 more dive bombers on the 24th, another two squadrons on the 30th, 24 fighters on 11 September. A scenario of those four weeks has to be able to say so.

## Decisions

1. **A scenario may list arrivals:** a day, an air group, a number of aircraft and a number of pilots.
2. **An arrival is delivered at the start of its day,** in the night, with the day's replacements and before them. The aircraft arrive ready. The pilots are added only if the group's pilots are tracked.
3. **An arrival raises the group's strength by its aircraft.** A squadron that flies in is part of the establishment from then on, and replacements can keep it up. A group may start the scenario with no aircraft and a strength of nothing and get both from its first arrival.
4. **A group with no aircraft is at its base all along.** It can be given orders before its aircraft come, and flies nothing until they do.
5. **An arrival on day 1 is the same as starting with the aircraft.** It is allowed, and delivered before anything flies.
6. **An arrival for a group that has been lost with its ship is not delivered.**

## Why

- **It is the same act as a replacement,** on one day and by name. The same step of the night delivers both, and the same event records both.
- **No new entity.** A squadron that is "on its way" is an air group at its destination with no aircraft yet. Nothing has to move it there.
- **Strength follows arrivals** so that a base reinforced in September is not held to the ceiling of August.

## What it costs

- A group cannot arrive at one base and later move to another. Moving groups is still deferred. *(Nov 14, 2026: rebasing is built by decision 0091, for land-based groups)*
- The flight in costs nothing: no wear, no fatigue, no supply, and nothing can intercept it.
