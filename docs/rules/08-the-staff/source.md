# The staff: The staff chooses a source of supply

Part of chapter 8, [The staff](README.md). How the staff chooses a source of supply (source.rs).

## The staff chooses a source of supply

Decision 0228, amended on review and by the architect's rulings on the build (doctrine `supply-59`; state layout `flagplot-state-v59`; saved game 27; agent interface 53; the rules label stays 32). It draws no random number and the staff reads only the side's own view and command state.

**The key.** A scenario states, for a side, that its staff chooses its sources of supply: `staff_chooses_supply_sources = true` in the side's table, `[side.allied]` or `[side.japanese]`, beside the side's other doctrine values (Patrolling waters with submarines, The pattern of a patrol; chapter 10). It is the side's own state, hashed with its command state, set when the game is made and changed by nothing after. Left out, or false, it is off, and **for a side without it nothing in this section applies**: the island chooses by the most supply above the reserve, a commander's hold with no source forms no supply and says which bases could be one, a run is cut to the stock above the reserve, and every order and reason is what `supply-58` gave. No file on file states it but the table's. [0228, A]

**One rule for choosing**, used under a commander's hold and under "control an island" for a side with the key:

1. A base may be chosen where it is the side's, is not in the hex of the place supplied (under "control an island", in no named place's hex), the staff may take it (Controlling an island, item 5 of decision 0149) and it has supply to spare.
2. **Supply to spare.** Days of supply are a hex's (Days of supply), so what a base keeps is worked for its hex: the greater of the summed supply reserves of the side's bases there and fifteen days (the supply doctrine's own interval, a placeholder) of the hex's planned use today. The hex's supply less that, not below 0, is what the hex can spare; a base can spare the lesser of its own supply above its own reserve and what its hex can spare.
3. **The place's need** is fifteen days of the planned use of the place's hex today (under an island, of its first place); 0 where the side uses nothing there yet.
4. **The order.** A base that can spare the place's need before one that cannot; then one where a transport force the staff may take lies stopped with nothing aboard before one with none; then the most to spare; then the nearer to the place by the engine's sea path; then the lower number. The reason carries, as evidence, each base weighed with its figures (what it can spare against the place's need, the force there, the miles) and names the source.
5. **The choice is kept.** On later mornings the source the staff set is the source while the base is the side's, whatever the stocks have become; the reason of the morning says it was chosen on an earlier morning and kept. The staff chooses again only when the base is no longer the side's.
6. **With no base that may be chosen** the staff forms no supply and says the place lives on what it has, with its days of supply, naming each base the staff could take that has nothing to spare with what it holds and keeps.

**Under a commander's hold** (Holding a place, The supply that follows), for a side with the key, on a morning the hold has men at its base and no other "keep supplied" for the hex is in force, the staff forms "keep supplied" under the hold (it shares the hold's plan) and, each morning it differs, sets that supply's forces limit: the source is the commander's where an intent of his on the chain names one (and none is chosen, whatever it holds and whatever else draws on it), else the staff's, chosen and kept; the ships are the commander's where his limits bind ships, else the transport forces stopped at the source with nothing aboard, a pool that only grows while the source is the same and is made anew when it changes. A source he names is taken up on the staff's next morning, and one he puts back to the staff's choice stays where it is. With no ship at the source the supply is formed all the same: it is served, says that no ship of its pool is afloat and sends nothing; the pool grows on the morning a force comes. **Under "control an island"** the source of the island's holds is chosen by the same rule; everything else of the island's source and pool is as it was (Controlling an island). For a base the commander names as an island's source, the island does not refuse it because an order higher in priority draws on it; it refuses a base before its day or not the side's.

**What a chosen source gives.** A run from a source the staff chose is cut to what the source can spare on the morning it is ordered, less what runs still loading there have yet to take (those added earlier the same morning included), where a run from a source the commander named is cut to the stock above its reserve as before. The reason of a cut run says the load is cut to what the source can spare. Where the supply cannot be met for want of what the source can spare, the reason of the day names each other base of the side, not in the place's hex and not before its day, that has supply to spare, with what it can spare (or that none can), and records that it speaks of the source, so that its link leads to "supplied from".

**Plans.** Where the ships of a supply are the staff's to find (no forces limit of the commander's on its chain binds ships), an empty pool does not mark the plan failed: the supply says no ship of its pool is afloat and sends nothing. Where his limit names the ships and none is afloat, the plan is marked failed as before. Where the source is the staff's choice and the base is no longer the side's, the plan is not marked failed either; the staff chooses again that morning.

**Three places in the engine** change for a side with the key and for none other: the key itself; the check that a forces limit narrows (The intent tree, A limit must narrow): a forces limit set on a "keep supplied" whose parent is a "hold a place" is checked as under a take, each limit above binding only what it names, a source the commander names above still binding; and the limit a run of a hold's plan is checked against (Runs): that of the lowest-numbered "keep supplied" in force directly under the hold that has one, in place of the hold's own.

**The tools.** `command_state` gives each hold and each island in force `supplied_from`: `{"base", "chosen_by"}`, `chosen_by` `staff` or `commander`, the source the supply under it reads (the nearest forces limit on its chain that names one) and who chose it (`commander` where an intent of his on the chain names a source); left out where the order reads no source (no supply is in force under it). It is worked from the command state and stored nowhere, and given for every side that has one to give (agent interface version 53). [0228]
