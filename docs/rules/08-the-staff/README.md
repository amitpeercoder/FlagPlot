# The staff

This chapter holds what a side's commander says as state, the commands that change it, the staff that a side may have, the reasons the staff gives, and days of supply. It is written by step 1 of stage 1 of step 3: the engine's half (command state, commands, the staff passed in, days of supply) and the staff's half (the crate `sim-staff`, the doctrine for keeping a base supplied, the reasons of staff commands, the staff's projection of days of supply, and the tools a seat commands with), step 2 adds priority between intents, commitments of stock and displaced demand, and step 3 adds holds, the override of a hold by the commander's own order, ending a run, the supply a free ship has aboard, the fallback of a cancelled plan, previews and the tools `hold`, `release` and `preview`, and step 4 adds interrupts and their acknowledgement, the staff's notices, a run of days that stops for an interrupt, the command report and the tools `set_interrupt`, `acknowledge`, `run` and `report`. Step 2 of stage 2 adds the intent tree (an intent has a parent, a maker and an addressee, and the staff forms sub-intents), the end "hold a place", the task "cover a base from the air" with the air doctrine that serves them, air groups in a forces limit, a hold on a whole air group, limits read up the tree and the measure of a hold intent. Step 3 of stage 2 adds the level a doctrine belongs to, the end "neutralise a base" with its doctrine at the area's level, the task "raid a base" with its doctrine at the base's level, and the cover's give of a raid's bomber and its escorts. Step 4 adds the end "deny an area" with its doctrine at the area's level, the tasks "strike ships from a base" and "search waters from a base" with their doctrines at the base's level, the size of a strike decided at the group's level, and the cover's arc over the waters to deny. The second and third parts of step 4 add the task force as a level that an intent is addressed to, the task "stand at a hex" with its doctrine at the task force's level and the rule of the engine it needs (the night stand, chapter 2), and the task "operate a carrier force" with its doctrine. Every side of every game has the staff; a side with no intent in force has a staff that does nothing, so a game with no command is played exactly as it was before this chapter. Step 8 of stage 3 adds the island, a scenario's own data, and the end "control an island", from which the staff draws a tree of the ends the earlier steps built, with its measure (Controlling an island, below). Stage 3 of the submarine rules (decision 0218) adds the end "patrol the waters with submarines" with its doctrine at the area's level, a hold on a boat, boats in a forces limit and the staff's regard for the waters a side suspects enemy submarines work in (Patrolling waters with submarines, below). Decision 0232 (step 1) lets the commander write part of a plan himself: a sub-intent under an intent of his own, with one pair open, a "take a place" under "control an island", which the staff then fills round, forms nothing beside and carries out (A sub-intent the commander states, below; Taking a place, Under "control an island"). [0107, 0111, 0115, 0116, 0117, 0120, 0123, 0124, 0125, 0151, 0218, 0232]

No rule here draws a random number. Every quantity is a fixed-point number (chapter 1, Numbers and chance).

## What is built and what is not

Built: command state (intents with their parent, maker and addressee, six limits, two leaves, plans with their states, baselines, amendments and runs, commitments of ships, holds, and interrupts); seventeen commands and who may give each; the staff passed in to the function that resolves a day, and what it hands in; the check of a staff's orders against the holds and against a forces limit; reasons; days of supply; the saved game of version 3, `replay --check-staff` and the doctrine version; a scenario's commands; the doctrine for keeping a base supplied, with its projection of days of supply and its proposals; priority between intents, what is committed the same morning, the protected limit, commitments of stock as runs and displaced demand (step 2); holds on a ship, a task force and the search arc of an air group, the override of a hold by the commander's own order (step 2 of the day), ending a run, the runs the staff sets aside for a held ship, the supply aboard a free ship counted toward a run's load, the fallback of a cancelled plan, and where a run lands in the projection (step 3); the tools of steps 1 and 3, with previews; and the interrupts of the commander, with the standing ones and their acknowledgement, tested each morning, the staff's notices, and the tools of step 4 (`set_interrupt`, `acknowledge`, `run` and `report`); and, from step 2 of stage 2, the intent tree, limits read up it, air groups in a forces limit, a hold on a whole air group, the end "hold a place" with its measure, the doctrine for covering a base from the air, and the chain of a reason; and, from step 3 of stage 2, the level of a reason, the end "neutralise a base" with the raid tasks it forms, and the raid doctrine; and, from step 4, the end "deny an area" with its measure, the strike-ships and search-waters tasks and their doctrines, and the level of a group in a reason; and, from the second and third parts of step 4, a task force as an addressee, the task "stand at a hex" with its doctrine and the night stand, and the task "operate a carrier force" with its doctrine; and, from step 6 of stage 2, ammunition as policy (a force short of ammunition does not sortie and is not a free escort); and, from step 2 of stage 3, the tasks "ready a landing" and "build up a base", the first doctrines that order land units, with land units in a forces limit and a land unit as a hold's target; and, from steps 3 and 4 of stage 3, the end "take a place", with the judgement of each morning ashore and a second lift, the check that a place can be held, a hold stated of a place not yet the side's, the men of a hold and the supply that follows it; and, from step 8 of stage 3, the island as map data, the end "control an island" with the tree the staff draws from it, a plan of their own for a take and a supply intent formed under it, the staff's withdrawing of what it formed, and the island's measure; and, from stage 3 of the submarine rules, the patrol order with its stations, a hold on a boat, a boat in a forces limit and the regard for submarine waters (decision 0218); and, from decision 0232, a sub-intent the commander states under his own island (a take a place), the staff's regard for it, and the marks that say whose node is whose in the tools. [0107, rulings 10 to 16, 17 to 30 and 31 to 37; 0115, 0116, 0117, 0120, 0123, 0124, 0125, 0127, 0141, 0151, 0218, 0232]

Not built, and so not rules: a hold on a base (it waits for a stage whose doctrine acts on a base); the withdrawal of a numbered interrupt and any condition but a base's days of supply; a notice the staff hands in on a day it weighs no new run (Notices, below); escort and the recall of a run; a second source; an intent for fuel; and a phase of a plan (below, Plans). Escort and the bombers of a strike intent, a land formation as the addressee of an intent, a commander stating a sub-intent of any pair but the one of decision 0232, the staff withdrawing a sub-intent, are not built either (Not built, below). [0107, rulings 17, 30 and 35; 0115, 0117]

## The parts of this chapter

The chapter is split by subject into the files below, in this order. The text of each section is as it was in the single file `08-the-staff.md`; a reference such as "(Holding a place, below)" names a section that may now be in another part, found by the list here. A doctrine with its own module in `crates/sim-staff/src` has its rules in the file named for it where the subject allows.

   Sections: Command state; The intent tree.
2. [Commands](commands.md): The commands that change command state, who may give each, and every reason one is refused.
   Sections: Commands.
