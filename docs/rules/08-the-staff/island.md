# The staff: Controlling an island

Part of chapter 8, [The staff](README.md). The doctrine that controls an island and the takes formed under it (island.rs).

## Controlling an island

Decision 0151, step 8 of stage 3, second part. The end a commander states alone, from which the staff draws the tree of ends the earlier steps built: take a place, hold a place, keep it supplied, deny the waters, operate the carriers and neutralise the enemy's bases near. Its state is one kind of intent; the engine gains three things for it, each the architect's addition on the build's finding: the staff's withdrawal of an intent it formed, plans of their own for three intents formed under an island (the third by decision 0231), and the reading of forces limits under one (below). It draws no random number. The staff reads only the side's own view: its bases, forces and units, its map of the enemy's bases, and every intent in force and its plan. [0151]

**An island** is a scenario's data. A `[[island]]` table has a `number`, a `name` and `places`, in order, each a base, `{ base = N }`, or a coast hex, `{ hex = [q, r] }`. A place that is a base means that base's hex. An island is on both sides' maps, as the map is: it is map data, it never changes in a game, and it is not state and not in the hash. The loader refuses an island with no place, a number used twice, a base place that names no base of the scenario, a hex place off the map or not a coast hex, and a place with both keys or neither. [0151 items 1 and 2]

**The end.** "Control an island" (`control_island`, code 15) names an island and may have a date, `by_day`. It is an end addressed to the area and a root. `set_intent` of an island that is not the scenario's is refused as "No such island" (Every reason a command is refused). It needs no forces limit. Its plan is drafted, prepared and released by the leaves, as a hold's is (Holding a place), and stands until the intent is withdrawn: **the intent is never marked complete**. Under it the staff may form a take a place, a hold a place, a deny an area and a neutralise a base, and under the denial an operate a carrier force; and the commander may write a take a place under it himself (A node the commander wrote, below). Each take the staff forms is formed with the island's `by_day`, and the rule of a take's date is decision 0142's. The staff sets no risk: each intent beneath reads the island's, up the tree. [0151 items 3, 4 and 7, 12]

**Plans of their own.** A tree has one plan, its root's, but for three intents formed under an island, each of which has a plan of its own, drafted when it is formed (`PlanDrafted`, after `IntentFormed`). Everything else formed under the island shares the island's plan. So the rule that a sub-intent serves its root's plan reads: the plan of the nearest intent on its chain that owns one. [0151 item 9]

| Intent | Its plan |
| --- | --- |
| A "take a place" under the island, formed by the staff or stated by the commander (decision 0232) | Drafted when it is formed or stated; for the staff's, made ready on leave to prepare the morning it is formed, for his, made ready on the leaves once the island's plan has been released (A node the commander wrote); released and marked complete by the take's doctrine as a root take's is (Taking a place) |
| A "keep supplied" formed under a "hold a place" that stands under the island | Made ready and released by the supply doctrine on the leaves, as a root supply intent's is. With no forces limit of its own, its forces limit and source, and those of a run, are read up the tree: the hold's, set by the staff |
| A "keep supplied" formed directly under the island, for a base beside a place (decision 0231) | Made ready and released by the supply doctrine on the leaves, as the one above's is. Its forces limit is its own, set by the staff the morning it is formed and each morning the source or the pool differs, and a run of its plan reads it first |

Withdrawing the island ends each such plan: complete where it was released, else cancelled. [0151 item 9]

**Forces limits under an island.** The commander may set one on the island or on anything beneath it, and the staff's choices are inside them. [0151 item 4]

- Each limit above an intent binds only what it names of the task forces (with ships), the land units and the source, as under a take. The narrowing check of decision 0149 is used under an island too.
- When a chain is read, the island's own limit fills what it does not name from the nearest limit beneath it, and with none beneath it is read as set.
- A forces limit that names no air group does not bind the air groups: a hold's cover and every doctrine under the island that uses the groups at a base use the groups based there as with no limit. A limit that names groups narrows them as before (decisions 0117 item 8, 0120 item 7, 0123 item 3).
- A take under an island with no limit of its own chooses inside the island's limit, and its staff limit then names the whole choice: the transport and other forces, the units and the source (Taking a place, Under "control an island").

**The tree each morning.** The doctrine runs right after the leaves and before every other air doctrine (The day's order). Each morning the island's plan is released the staff forms what is missing, by `form_intent`, in the island's place order and in this order. An intent of the same kind and place in force anywhere on the side, whoever made it, counts: none is formed beside it, and where it stands in another tree the reason names it, with its number and whose it is. [0151 items 5 and 6]

| Order | Formed | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1, for each named place in the island's order | **Take a place**, with the island's date | The side holds no base at the place, no take of it is in force with a plan that has not ended, and no take of it that the commander wrote under the island is in force with a plan not complete (A node the commander wrote) |
| 2, for each named place | **Hold a place** | Of the side's lowest-numbered base at the place; else the enemy's lowest-numbered base there on the side's map, which waits until it is taken (Holding a place, A place not yet the side's). A bare hex has no hold until the side has a base there |
| Between 2 and 3 (decision 0231), for each base in base order | **Keep supplied** a base beside a place, with a plan of its own | Men beside a place, below |
| 3 | **Deny an area**, once | Within 3 hexes of the island's first place. The 3 hexes are a placeholder |
| 4, under the denial | **Operate a carrier force**, for each force | Each carrier force the staff may take (decision 0149, item 5) that has a carrier afloat with a group aboard, at the covering hex of decision 0148, item 2, reckoned to the island's first place (Operating a carrier force, item 20) |
| 5 | **Neutralise a base**, for each enemy base in base order | The enemy base is on the side's map within 15 hexes of a named place. The 15 hexes are the placeholder of decision 0124 |
| 6, in the bombard step, under the first neutralising of an enemy base at a named place | **Bombard a base**, for each force | Each force on the bombardment mission the staff may take, with a ship afloat, stopped at a base of the side (The bombardment forces, below) |

Under one root a unit or a force serves the first intent, in this order, that takes it that morning, and a ship serves the run or the lift that has it until it is back. The rule of what the staff may take (decision 0149, item 5) keeps a unit, a force or a base from an intent of another root only; under one island it keeps as well what a landing has from the takes beside it, and what a commander's take names from the others (Taking a place, Under "control an island", a take the commander wrote, item 4), and a supply run takes no force that is readying a landing (differences.md, rows 85 and 86). What follows is the built doctrines': each take is prepared, launched on leave and landed; each hold puts its men on the defence, covers its base and forms its supply; the check that a place can be held is made before a take is launched; the denial and the neutralising use what their doctrines find and say so where they find nothing. A base in a named place's hex that a take of this tree is to take is neutralised too, until it is taken. [0151 items 5 to 9]

**A node the commander wrote** (decision 0232). A "take a place" the commander stated under the island (A sub-intent the commander states, in The intent tree) is in the island's tree, his, and is served by the take's doctrine (Taking a place, Under "control an island", a take the commander wrote). What the island does about it: [0232, rules 9, 14 and 16]

- **It forms nothing beside it.** While a commander's take of a named place is in force under the island and its plan is not complete, the island forms no take of that place; so too where he cancelled its plan. (For a take the staff formed, and for a take in another tree, the island passes over a place only while a take of it has a plan that has not ended, as before.) A plan marked complete is a landing carried out: his node stands as done, as a staff's take does, and if the place is later lost the staff retakes it by the rule as it is, with a take of its own. Where his take stands, the island's reason of the morning says so in a line, where a take of the island's own tree stands without a word: "intent:K, the commander's, plans the place at H: the staff forms no take beside it." [0232, rule 9]
- **Its date is read** by the island as the date of the take (Taking a place, Under "control an island", a take the commander wrote, item 4): a date later than the island's is carried out and the island's plan is told it is late. [0232, rule 14]
- **The two notices it hands in** for the island's plan are the `late` and the `cannot_meet` of the table in that item, in two cases they were not handed in for it before: his take's date is later than the island's, and he cancelled his take's plan and the place is not the side's. Each is once for its kind and its plan. [0232, rule 14]
- **A node he withdraws** is a blank again: the island forms the take that morning. [0232, rule 15]

What each doctrine does with a node of his, for the one pair of this build: [0232, rule 16]

| Doctrine | What it does with a commander's take under an island |
| --- | --- |
| The island | Forms no take of the place; forms the hold of the place, the denial, the carriers under it, the neutralising and the bombardment as before, and they serve his take as they serve a staff's; hands in `late` and `cannot_meet` as above. Its source of supply, its pools and what it withdraws read only the holds, supplies and neutralisings the staff formed, none of which he can write in this build |
| Taking a place | Serves it; reads its date, the limit it chooses within, what his other nodes name and what the other landings under the island have |
| The leaves | Pass it over while the island's plan is a draft or preparing |
| Told to wait, the need to know | Read his limits on it and above it, as on a root take |
| Readying a landing | Serves the task the staff formed beneath it, as before |
| Carriers under the island | The strike on the place is for the first take of the tree whose plan is released, his or the staff's. No carrier task is formed under his take, for cover or to look |
| Bombarding a base | Forms none under his take; the island's bombardment under its neutralising is as before |
| Holding a place, the check that a place can be held | By hex, as before |
| The plan order | The island's order lists his take as his (The plan order) |
| Supply | Takes no ship of a force that is readying a landing, his or the staff's |
| The air doctrines, the patrol | Nothing: they do not read a take |

**The staff withdraws.** On the first morning a base the staff is neutralising under the island is the side's, and before the tree is formed that morning, the staff withdraws the intent it formed for it (`withdraw_intent` by the staff, on an intent the staff made). The supply of a base beside a place is withdrawn too, on the conditions of Men beside a place, below. [0151 item 8b; 0231, 6]

**Men beside a place** (decision 0231, as amended on review; rules label 34, doctrine `supply-61`). The men of a side on the island's land at a base of the side beside a named place are the island's to feed. No random number is drawn, no state is added and the hash does not move: what it forms is intents, which are state already. The staff reads only the side's own bases, units and intents, the map and its land links.

1. **The island's land** is the hexes of its named places and every hex joined to one of them by land links, through any number of hexes. It is read from the map, which both sides hold; it is not state.
2. **A base beside a place** is a base of the side on the island's land, in no named place's hex, that the staff may take (decision 0149, item 5) and that is not the island's source of supply, and in whose hex the side has men ashore, of any unit, fighting or working, by whatever way they came there, or to whose hex a unit of the side is marching (both read from the side's own units: a unit that is beaten or ordered to march is on its old hex's record until it arrives).
3. **It is kept supplied.** Each morning, after the takes and the holds are formed and before the denial, for each such base in base order with no "keep supplied" for a base in its hex in force anywhere on the side, the staff forms one under the island by `form_intent`: with the floor of the island's risk in days, never less than 1 (10 at preserve, 5 at balanced, 1 at press; decision 0230) and no interval, so that the staff uses 15 days, and with a plan of its own, made ready and released by the supply doctrine on the leaves. A "keep supplied" for the hex in another tree counts, and the reason names it as an intent of another tree is named. The supply doctrine is asked before the island's tree is formed, so an intent formed on a morning is first served the next.
4. **Its source and its ships are the island's.** Its forces limit is its own: the staff sets it the morning it forms it, and each morning that the island's source or pool differs, to the island's source of supply and the transport forces stopped there with nothing aboard, with those it named before while they have a ship afloat (The source kept, and its pool); for a side whose staff chooses its sources, by that rule. With no source it forms none and says so, as a hold does.
5. **The order of service is the contract's.** The supplies under one island have the island's rank, so they are served in the order of their numbers; a supply of a base beside a place formed on the first day is served before the supply of men landed later, and a run of it under way keeps its ships until it is back. No second order of service puts a named place first.
6. **It ends.** On the first morning that all of these hold, the staff withdraws the intent it formed: no man of the side is ashore in the hex, no unit of the side is marching to it, and no run of its plan is loaded or at sea. Until then it stays in force and the supply doctrine brings a run home as any is. With no man in the hex the planned use is nothing, and the doctrine forms no new run. The engine marks a released plan of its own complete on the withdrawal. A unit that goes and comes back has the supply withdrawn on the morning none is there and formed again, with a new number and a new plan, on the morning it is marching back: an intent a time, one a morning at most.
7. **It gives no order to the men.** They stay as they are, or under whatever order they have. Whether they are marched back, joined to a take or taken off is not this rule's.
8. **Men at a hex with no base of the side** cannot be fed: a unit is fed through a base in its hex, and a unit that falls back to a bare hex forms none. For each hex of the island's land with men of the side ashore and no base of the side, each morning, the staff gives no order and records one reason: the hex, the units and their men, that no base of the side stands there to feed them through, and that it would change with a base there or the men moved. For a unit that is marching the reason names the hex it is recorded in, that it carries nothing and where it marches to. No notice and no interrupt is added for it.
9. **Only under "control an island".** A "take a place" or a "hold a place" stated alone names one place, and the men it feeds are those of Holding a place, Men fed before it is taken. Nothing of them changes.
10. **What the staff says.** The reason of the forming names the base, its hex, the men ashore there by unit, the supply the base holds and its days at today's use, the place of the island it is beside and that it would change with no man there. The plan order of the island lists the bases so fed among its tasks, and the text of the `plan_order` answer says "It feeds beside its places:" with each base, its hex and the place it is beside (agent interface version 55 stays).

The engine changes in three places, and the rules label is raised for them: a "keep supplied" may stand under "control an island" where its base is the side's; so formed it has a plan of its own; and its floor may be 0. A side sees its own intents, plans, reasons, orders and notices of this, whole; the enemy sees none of it. [0231]

**The source of supply** (for a side whose staff chooses its sources, by the one rule of The staff chooses a source of supply) of the island's holds is the commander's, where the island's forces limit names one, whatever it holds. Else it is the side's base that is in no named place's hex and that the staff may take, with the most supply above its reserve; then the lower number. A base with nothing above its reserve cannot be the source, and no transport force need lie there. On the morning it forms a hold, the staff sets that hold a forces limit of the source and the transport forces stopped there with nothing aboard, which may be none. With no such base it sets none, says so in its reason, and the hold forms no supply (Holding a place, The supply that follows). With no ship in the limit, a "keep supplied" formed under the hold is served and says that no ship of its pool is afloat, and sends nothing. [0151 item 8; 0163 items 1 to 3]

**The source kept, and its pool.** Where the commander names no source, the source is chosen on the first morning a base fits, which with any base of the side holding supply above its reserve is the first morning of the island, and kept: on later mornings the source of a hold the staff formed under the island, while it is the side's base, is the source. Each morning the staff sets the forces limit of each hold it formed under the island to the source and the transport forces it may take that lie stopped there with nothing aboard, where that differs from the limit the hold has. The pool is the transport forces stopped at the source with nothing aboard (no supply, fuel or man in any ship); on the morning the source is named it may be none. A force found there on a morning joins by a command of that morning, and a "keep supplied" served that morning reads the limit as it stood, so its first run with that force is ordered the morning after. The lifts that have landed go on to the source to join it (Taking a place, Forces with men aboard, item 5). The pool only grows: a force once named stays while it has a ship afloat, so that a ship that serves a run, loaded or under way, stays inside the limit its orders are checked against; a convoy the side may use from a later day joins the pool on that day. [0152 items 10 and 11; 0163 items 1, 2 and 5; the growing pool, the architect's ruling of Nov 18, 2026]

**The carriers** are the island's. A take under an island, the commander's too, takes no carrier force, and the force is not sent home when a take is met. The carrier tasks under the island's denial are served after the landing's morning, with the takes' carrier tasks, by the carrier doctrine as it is; and the strike on the place applies to such a task for the first take of the tree (Operating a carrier force, Under "control an island"). [0151 item 8a]

**The bombardment forces** are the island's too. A take under an island, the commander's too, takes no force on the bombardment mission. Each morning, in the bombard step (Bombarding a base), under the island's **first neutralising of an enemy base at a named place** (for each place in the island's order, the neutralising in force under the island of the lowest-numbered enemy base on the side's map in its hex), the staff forms "bombard a base" for each task force on the bombardment mission with a ship afloat that the staff may take (decision 0149, item 5; inside the island's forces limit where it names task forces) and that lies stopped at a base of the side, in task force order, where no bombard task for that force is in force anywhere on the side. A force the staff may not take, one before its day among them, is named with the reason; a force that may be used only from a later day is so taken on that day's morning. A bombard task the island's own tree formed stands without a word; one in another tree is named. No other neutralising under the island takes a bombardment force, so a base off the island is not bombarded by this. The task is then the bombard doctrine's as it is: whether it sorties, its speed, its fuel and its rounds. When the base is taken, the neutralising is withdrawn (The staff withdraws) and the bombard task with it. [0151 item 8c]

**Its measure.** It is worked from the side's own view and events, stored nowhere, as a hold's measure is (Holding a place, Its measure), and no rule reads it. A day is **controlled** when, at its end, every named place is the side's (a place that names a base is the side's when that base is: a base formed by a landing beside the enemy's does not make it so; a bare hex is when the side holds a base there), and in each place the summed supply of the side's bases there is above the reserves of those bases, and the side's days of supply in the hex is at least the floor of the "keep supplied" under the place's hold (5 days, a placeholder, where there is none). A place holds a base of the side on a day where the side's own events show `BaseAtDayEnd` that day for a base in the hex. The days of supply are the stock over today's daily use of the hex, and today's use is applied to past days. [0151 items 10 to 12]

| Part | What |
| --- | --- |
| Days in force | As for a hold's measure |
| Days controlled | Of those days, the controlled days |
| Each place | Its hex, whether it is the side's, and its days of supply today |

It is shown in `command_state` (as `measure` on the island intent) and in `report` (as an entry of `islands`), with the words "controlled on N of M days in force", then each place with its hex, whether it is the side's and its days of supply today. [0151 item 11]

A side sees: its own `IntentFormed`, `PlanDrafted`, staff reasons, orders, notices and the measure, whole. The enemy sees none of it. [0151]
