# The staff: Landings, taking a place and the plan order

Part of chapter 8, [The staff](README.md). Readying a landing and building up a base; taking a place; the plan order (take.rs, take/, ready.rs, order.rs).

## Readying a landing, and building up a base

Decision 0141, step 2 of stage 3. Two tasks a commander may state as roots, the first doctrines that order land units. Each is served each morning in the air doctrine's run, after the carrier forces, by the level it is addressed to: a "ready a landing" by its task force, a "build up a base" by its base. Its numbers are the ground figures (chapter 7); with none loaded it gives no order and says so. The staff reads only the side's own view.

**Leaves.** A plan of either kind takes leave to prepare as a hold's does (a draft waits for it, with a reason and the notice `waits_for_leave_to_prepare`), and works while it is preparing or released. It never asks leave to launch and the staff never releases it: it launches nothing. Nothing is ordered without leave to prepare. [0141 items 5 and 15]

**The units a task may use.** Those named in every forces limit on its chain (The six limits, above). With none on the chain, a "build up a base" may use the working units of the side with men ashore in its base's hex, and a "ready a landing" none. The same test is intake's (The forces limit on a staff's orders) and the override's. Each morning the tasks of both kinds are served in priority order (ranked first, then by number), and each leaves out, with words in its reasons: a unit before its day (A unit before its day, above); a unit that is held, "the staff plans without it"; a unit a higher task took this morning, "serves intent:N, which is higher in priority"; a unit with no man left; and, for a build-up, a unit not ashore in the base's hex. A unit serves one task. [0141 items 8, 13, 14 and 16]

**Ready a landing.** Each morning:

1. **The force's order.** Where the force's preparation is not for the task's hex with exactly the units kept as its roster, the staff gives the force `prepare_landing` for the hex with those units (chapter 7, Preparing a landing). A force that is held is given none, and a force before its day is given none and the task gets one reason. A working unit kept is on the roster and is given no order. [0141 items 2 and 4]
2. **Each fighting unit on the roster** is set to train or to stand ready (which on a roster is to rehearse), where its activity differs. Below the standard of experience, 2, it trains at preserve the force until it is at the standard; at balanced until it is at the standard, or, with a date, until the days left before the date (the date less today) are no more than the days its rehearsal still needs; at press it does not train. A unit at or above the standard rehearses, and so does one that cannot train: it has men aboard, its hex holds no base of its side, or its experience is at the training ceiling. The days a rehearsal needs are what is left to 100 of its rehearsal for the task's hex over its day's gain (`preparation_per_day` times its assault factor), rounded up. [0141 item 3]
3. **Ready.** The task is ready on a morning the force's preparation is already for the hex with that roster, the roster is not empty, and the force's loading and each fighting unit's rehearsal for the hex are at least 100 (50 at press), and at preserve the force each fighting unit is at the standard. On the first such morning the staff gives no order and hands in the notice `ready`, once. [0141 item 5]
4. **Late.** With a date, on a morning the task is not ready, the staff projects the morning it would be: today plus the longest of the days the loading needs (from the loading the force will have after today's order, at `preparation_per_day`) and, for each fighting unit, its days of training by the rule of item 2 (the days left to the standard, `training_days_per_level` a level less those counted) and then of rehearsal to the risk's mark. If that morning is after the date, or a unit can never be ready, it hands in the notice `late`, once, with the days late and what binds: the loading, a unit's training (where its days include training) or a unit's rehearsal; at a tie the loading, then the lower unit. [0141 item 6]
5. **Reasons** give each unit's experience against the standard, its days of training counted against the days a level takes, its rehearsal for the hex and the days it still needs at its gain, the force's loading, the risk and the date. A morning with no order for the task has one reason that says so. [0141 item 7]

**Build up a base.** Each morning every unit the task may use, at the base, is set to work at the job of the first want not yet met, where its activity differs: an airfield and its damage mended at `airfield`, defences at `defences`, the port at `port`, aircraft repair at `aircraft`. An airfield is met when the base has one; its damage mended when its airfield has no damage (a base with no airfield has none); defences when the base's defences are at the number or above. The port and aircraft repair are never met: they are worked while they are the first want left. With every want met the staff gives no order and says so, and on the first such morning hands in the notice `met`, once; a want that stops being met is worked again. The reasons give the work still to do on the want (for an airfield `airfield_work` less the work done; for mending the damage times `work_per_airfield_damage_point`; for defences the work at each grade's cost from the defences to the number), the work the units make in a day (chapter 7, The day's work, with the fighting share of the last night: its fed share where the figures of decision 0208 are not given, so a day's count for a unit that is mending is a little long, since it is worked on today's share), and the days that comes to, rounded up. A fighting unit is put to work only where a forces limit names it. [0141 items 8 to 12]

**Once.** A notice is handed in once for its plan: the staff reads its own side's events and hands in no notice of a kind it handed in for the plan before. [0141 items 5, 6 and 11]

A side sees: its own orders, reasons and notices, whole. The enemy sees none. [0141]

## Taking a place

Decision 0142, step 3 of stage 3. An end a commander states, served by the area each morning in the air doctrine's run: its preparing before the tasks that ready units are served, and its launching after them. It carries a landing as far as the day the place is the side's; what the men do ashore when the enemy has men there is step 4. Its numbers are the ground figures (chapter 7); with none loaded it gives no order and says so. The staff reads only the side's own view: the enemy as its public bases and the side's estimate of its men.

**The end.** "Take a place" (`take_place`, code 13) names a hex of land, coast or inland, and may have a date: the day by the end of which the place is to be the side's. A tool may name the place by a base on the side's map that is not the side's, which means that base's hex. It is a root, addressed to the area. Its forces limit may name the task forces, the land units and a source, a base of the side. Each of the three is the commander's where he names it and the staff's where he does not (With no forces named, below), and the end may have no forces limit at all: a forces limit with no source is not refused, as it is for keeping a base supplied. It is met when the side holds a base in the hex and the enemy holds none there. [0142 items 1 to 3; 0149 items 1 and 2]

**Its phase** is read, not stored: prepare while the plan is preparing; move from its release until a man of the side is ashore at the place; land from then until the intent is met. `command_state` shows it. [0142 item 5]

**Leaves.** A draft waits for leave to prepare as a hold's does. A draft whose take waits for its men (Waits for its men, below) is not made ready that morning, though leave to prepare is given: it stays a draft and is made ready on the morning the take forms its landing. A plan that is preparing is not released by the leaves: the end's own rule asks leave to launch, below. Nothing is ordered without leave to prepare, and nothing embarks or sails before the plan is released. [0142 items 15 and 25]

**Met.** On each morning, first: where the place is the side's, the staff marks the plan complete (`mark_plan`) and hands in the notice `met`, once (its words the intent's, "intent:N is met"), and orders nothing else. Stated of a place that is the side's already, that is the first morning. [0142 items 3 and 23]

**Prepare.** On the first morning the plan is approved and no "ready a landing" stands under it, the staff, where the end's forces limit names all three of the task forces, the land units and the source (otherwise With no forces named, below):

1. **Lands at the place itself.** For a place that is not a coast hex it gives no order and says it has no way there. [0142 item 4]
2. **Finds the transport force:** the lowest-numbered task force of the forces limit on the transport mission, with a ship afloat of a class that carries men, that the side may use, that the commander does not hold, and that lies stopped at the source or has men of the limit's units aboard. With none it forms nothing and says why for each. [0142 items 6 and 26]
3. **Finds the units that may land:** the fighting units of the forces limit that the side may use, that the commander does not hold, and whose men are all ashore in the source's hex or all aboard the transport force. A working unit is left out. Their order: the higher assault skill first, then the higher experience, then the lower number. [0142 items 7, 8 and 26]
4. **Gives them ships.** A ship carries men of one unit. A unit aboard keeps the ships it is in. In that order, each unit ashore takes the force's ships that carry men and are not yet taken, in number order, until their berths hold its men; a unit for which the ships left are too few is left out, and the next is tried. Which ship each unit's men go into is the load order's (chapter 7). [0142 item 9]
5. **Sizes them.** Where the side holds an estimate of the enemy's men at the place, it stops taking units once the men chosen are at least the estimate times 3 at preserve the force, 2 at balanced, 1 at press (the staff's figures, placeholders). With no estimate it takes every unit the ships can carry, and says that it goes with no count of the enemy. Where the units the ships can carry are fewer than the men wanted, it waits for its men (Waits for its men, below) where a day in reach brings them, at preserve the force and at balanced; otherwise at press it goes with what it has and says so, at balanced it hands in `cannot_bring_enough` (below), and at preserve the force it forms nothing, asks no leave, and hands in the notice `cannot_meet`, once. [0142 item 10; 0159 items 6 and 7]
6. **Forms the task** "ready a landing" under the end, for the transport force at the place's hex (`form_intent`), and sets its forces limit to the units chosen (`set_limit`). The choice is made once and kept as that limit, which the commander may change as he may any. With a date, the task's date is the morning of the end's day, less the days to embark and the days of the passage, plus one, and not before day 1. The task is served that morning (Readying a landing, above). [0142 items 11 to 13]

**Waits for its men.** Where the units chosen are short of the men wanted, none of their men is aboard, and the risk is preserve the force or balanced, the staff works the first day on which the units and forces it may then use, at the source, give the men wanted: for each day after today in turn it makes the choice again, Prepare, items 2 to 5 (or With no forces named, items 1 to 5), reading which units and forces the side may use on that day instead of today (A unit before its day, above), and nothing more of the enemy than the estimate it already reads. A day is in reach where it is no later than today plus 7 days (the staff's figure, a placeholder, `WAIT_HORIZON_DAYS`) for an end with no date; for an end with a date, where a landing formed that day, with that day's units, would be ready by the date its task would be given (Prepare, item 6), by the projection of Readying a landing (the days of loading and of each unit's training and rehearsal, at least one), the arithmetic of the notice `late`. [0159 items 4 and 6]

- **A day in reach brings them.** The staff waits for the first such day: it forms nothing and gives no order under the end that morning, and its reason says the men wanted, the men to hand, which unit or force may be used from which day, the men that day's lift gives, and that it waits for that day. It hands in no notice. The plan, where it is a draft, stays one (Leaves, above). On that day the choice gives the men wanted and the take goes on as Prepare says. [0159 item 6]
- **None does.** At preserve the force it is as Prepare, item 5, says. At balanced it does not go with what it has: it forms nothing, gives no order and hands in the notice `cannot_bring_enough`, once for the plan; each morning after, the reason says why. Its reason gives the men wanted, the men to hand, and each unit or force named (or, with none named, of the side) that may be used later with its day and why it does not serve: too late to land by the date, beyond the last day looked at, or not enough with it; or that none is coming. It waits for the commander, who may set the risk to press, change a limit or the date, or leave it; a new estimate or a unit's day coming into reach is read the next morning. [0159 item 7]
- Waiting for men aboard a force, a later lift (A second lift, Forces with men aboard, below), readying the men to hand while it waits, and landing at a beach away from the enemy are not in it: a lift with men aboard, a later lift and a second lift are under Not to starve, below. [0159 items 6 and 8]

**Not to starve.** At balanced, on a morning the staff would form or send a lift of men under the end, it lands men short of the men wanted, with too few in reach, only where it can feed them (decision 0169). `crates/sim-staff/src/take.rs`, `starve_check`.

1. **Where it is worked.** The first lift where any unit chosen is aboard (a lift as it stands among them), worked in Prepare before the task is formed; each later lift as it stands, worked as it is sent (Forces with men aboard, item 3); and a second lift (A second lift, below), worked on each morning it would be given an order or its units added to the limit. A first lift whose men are all ashore at its source is under Waits for its men, above, unchanged. A landing already formed is not stopped by it at its release. [0169 item 1]
2. **Short.** The lift is short where the fit men of the operation ashore at the place that can attack (Ashore, items 1 to 3, below) and the fighting men the lift carries are together fewer than the men wanted (Prepare, item 5). With no estimate held nothing is short and the lift goes as before. [0169 item 2]
3. **Nothing in reach.** The men in reach are those of item 2 and the fighting men aboard every other transport force the end may send as a later lift as it stands (Forces with men aboard, item 3), counted where the force may first be used on or before the end's last day: its date where one is set, else today and the 7 days after it, the horizon of Waits for its men. Where they are the men wanted or more, the lift goes as before. [0169 item 3]
4. **Fed.** Where the lift is short and the men in reach are too few, the men it lands can be fed where both hold: the place has a hold of a base in its hex with a source (Holding a place, The check), and the check that the place can be held, worked for this lift's force and every man aboard it, finds nothing short; and the supply the lift will have aboard when it sails, what is aboard and what it will load at its source, is at least the check's daily need times its days until supply follows, without the margin. With no such hold nothing follows the men, and they cannot be fed. [0169 item 4]
5. **Not fed.** The end forms no landing for that lift and gives its force no order; a later lift is not added to the limit, nor the units of a second lift. It hands in `cannot_bring_enough` once for the plan, and waits for the commander. Its reason gives the men wanted, the men the lift gives, the men in reach and the day reckoned to, and what of item 4 failed with its figures. Each later morning it reads all of it again, and goes on the first on which the lift is not short, enough men are in reach or the men can be fed. A plan not lifted stays as it was: a draft made ready on leave to prepare stays preparing. The men stay where they are. [0169 items 5 and 9]
6. **Fed: it goes** as before, and its reason says it goes short and that the men can be fed where they wait. [0169 item 6]
7. **The risk.** At press the lift goes. At preserve the force the end is as it was: Prepare, item 5, and the check of Holding a place stop it. [0169 items 7 and 8]

**With no forces named.** Where the end's forces limit does not name all three, or there is none, the staff chooses what the commander left out, on the same first morning, inside what he named, in the order a staff works in. `crates/sim-staff/src/assemble.rs`. [0149]

1. **What it may take.** A task force, a land unit or a base of the side that the side may use that day (A unit before its day, above), that the commander does not hold, and that is not named in the forces limit (read as it binds) of an intent in force served before the end that morning, in another tree, nor the task force a task of one is addressed to. A base so named is one that is that intent's source. [0149 item 5]
2. **The enemy.** The side's estimate of the men at the place and the men wanted by the risk, as in Prepare, item 5. With no estimate, as many as can be carried. [0149 item 6]
3. **The source.** A base can be the source if it is the side's, the staff may take it (and it is the commander's source, where he named one), a transport force it may take lies stopped there with a ship afloat that carries men, and a fighting unit it may take has all its men ashore in its hex and none aboard. For each such base, in base order, the staff works the lift it could make: the transport force of item 5 and the units of item 4 there. The passage is the engine's forecast for that force at cruising speed to the place. The source is the base whose lift gives the men wanted with the shortest passage, then the lower number; where none gives them, the base whose lift gives the most men, then the lower number; with no estimate, the most men, then the shortest passage, then the lower number. [0149 item 7]
4. **The units,** of those at the source it may take (and the commander named, where he named any), in the order and by the ships of Prepare, items 3 to 5, until the men wanted. [0149 item 8]
5. **The transport force,** of those it may take stopped at the source (and the commander named, where he named any): the lowest-numbered whose berths hold the men wanted; where none does, or with no estimate, the one with the most berths, then the lowest numbered. One force lands an operation. [0149 item 9]
6. **The risk on the way.** Every force on the bombardment mission that it may take that lies stopped at a base of the side, every force on the carrier mission that it may take wherever it lies, and every force on the escort mission that it may take that lies stopped at the source, is taken. They are given their tasks by the rules as they are: a bombardment task by Bombarding a base, a carrier task by Operating a carrier force, Under take a place, and the escort by Escorting and covering a run. [0149 items 10 and 11]
7. **The limit kept.** The staff forms the task "ready a landing" for the transport force at the place, as in Prepare, item 6, and sets its forces limit to what it chose of what the commander did not name: the transport force and the forces of item 6, where he named no task force; the units chosen, where he named no unit, or where it chose other units than those he named; and the source, where he named none. With all three named it is decision 0142's limit, the units chosen alone. [0149 item 3]
8. **Read as it binds.** Wherever the rules of this end read its forces limit for the task forces, the land units or the source (the transport force, the units and the source of the move and the second lift, the bombardment and carrier tasks, the escort), they read the commander's where he named it and the staff's limit on the "ready a landing" where he did not; and a forces limit on an intent under the end reads what it does not name of the three from the end, so read. The engine reads it so too, for the staff's orders, and a staff's `set_limit` on the task it formed may name under the end any of the three the end's limit does not name. [0149 item 3]
9. **What it could not find.** The reason of the choice gives each step in that order with its numbers: the estimate and the men wanted; each base that could be the source with its transport force, its berths, the men its lift gives and the hours of its passage, each base that could not and what it lacks, and the source and why; what the staff may not take and why (a unit or force that serves a higher intent is named with that intent); the units and the berths; where the lift falls short of the men wanted, by how many men; and the forces to bombard, to cover and to escort, or that it has none. With no base that can be the source the staff forms nothing, hands in `cannot_meet` once, and gives the reason each morning, with what is missing: a transport force it may take stopped at a base of the side, or a fighting unit beside it. With fewer men than wanted it does as Prepare, item 5, and Waits for its men: it waits for the day that brings them where one is in reach (at preserve the force and at balanced); otherwise at preserve the force it forms nothing and hands in `cannot_meet` once, at balanced it hands in `cannot_bring_enough` once, and at press it goes with what it has and says so. A lift as it stands is never waited for. A lack of a force to bombard, cover or escort stops nothing beyond what those rules already say. [0149 items 12 to 15]
10. **Chosen again.** On the first count of the enemy (The need to know, item 3) the units are chosen again at the source chosen and for the transport force of the task formed, from the units the end's limit binds and, where the commander named no unit, every fighting unit the staff may take ashore at the source. The source and the force stand: the task formed is the force's. [0149 item 4]

**Under "control an island".** A "take a place" that the staff forms under an island (Controlling an island, below) is the end of this section with these differences. [0151 items 4, 8a, 8c and 9]

- **A plan of its own.** It is drafted when the take is formed (`PlanDrafted`, after `IntentFormed`), is made ready on leave to prepare on the morning the take is formed, and is released and marked complete by this doctrine as a root take's is. It is not the island's plan; the island's plan ends it when the island is withdrawn (complete where released, else cancelled).
- **No carrier force and no bombardment force.** The take takes none (With no forces named, item 6 is set aside for carriers and for forces on the bombardment mission): the carrier tasks stand under the island's denial (Operating a carrier force, Under control an island), and the bombard tasks under its neutralising (Controlling an island, The bombardment forces); where a force on the bombardment mission lies stopped at a base of the side, the reason says "the bombardment forces are the island's, under its neutralising". The escort forces are taken as before. [0151 items 8a and 8c]
- **It chooses inside the island's limit.** With no forces limit of its own it chooses, by With no forces named, from what the island's limit (read as Controlling an island, Forces limits under an island, says) allows; its staff limit, on the "ready a landing", then names the whole choice: the transport force and the other forces, the units and the source.
- **Two takes under one island** keep from each other what each landing has (below).

**Under "control an island", a take the commander wrote** (decision 0232, step 1; rules label 35, doctrine `supply-63`). A "take a place" the commander stated under the island (A sub-intent the commander states, above) is served by the doctrine of this section as the staff's own take under an island is, with nothing changed but the six things below. Where a thing is said of "his take", it is said of a take the commander made under an island and of no other intent, so that no take that exists without him is touched, but for the two mends marked. [0232, rules 10 to 13 and 15]

1. **Served as the staff's take is.** Its plan of its own is made ready on leave to prepare; a "ready a landing" is formed under it by the staff; the men are sized against the estimate by the risk; the waits, the need to know, the check that the place can be held, leave to launch, the move, the landing, the mornings ashore and a second lift are as above. It has no carrier force and no bombardment force of its own, since those are the island's. [0232, rule 10]
2. **Nothing is done under it while the island's plan is a draft or preparing.** A staff's take cannot exist before the island's plan is released, and his can. The step of the leaves passes his take over on such a morning: its plan stays a draft and one reason says it waits for the island's plan. The wait is the staff's: a plan of his take that he approves by his own hand is served from that morning, the island's plan released or not. Once the island's plan has been released the wait is over for good: if he later cancels the island's plan, his take goes on as a staff's take under that island does. A preview or a proposal of his take reads the island's plan as released, as it reads the take's own. [0232, rule 10]
3. **A force on the carrier or the bombardment mission named in its forces limit is not used by it.** The cover and the look of a take form a carrier task for each carrier force its nearest forces limit names, and the bombard step likewise; the staff's own limit under an island never names one. For his take these three form nothing and the force stays the island's to use by its own rule. The take's reason of the morning says "task_force:N is on the carrier mission: under an island the carriers are the island's, and it is not this landing's", and so for the bombardment mission. The bombard step's case arises only where an enemy base lies in the take's hex, since a take with none has no target and forms no bombard task; where one does, his take is passed over in the forming of the bombard tasks, which reads the same nearest limit as the cover. [0232, rule 10]
4. **The staff fills his blanks and only his blanks.** Five parts. [0232, rule 11]
   - *Forces, kind by kind.* What his forces limit names of the task forces, the land units and the source is his; what it does not name the staff chooses, inside the island's limit, and keeps on the "ready a landing" it forms. The staff adds no unit and no force of a kind he named. The limit the staff chooses within, for his take, is made kind by kind, of the task forces, the land units and the source: his where his limit names it, else the island's where the island's names it, else the staff's own choice. One reading serves the first choice, the choice made again (With no forces named, item 10) and the later lifts. (For a take the staff formed, on which the commander has set a limit of his own under an island with a limit, a take with a limit of its own is read by that limit alone and the island's is not looked at: differences.md, row 84, not mended.)
   - *What a landing has is kept from the takes beside it.* This is for every take under an island, whoever made it, and mends the code to decision 0151, item 5 (differences.md, row 85). Under one island a unit or a force serves the first take that has it: for a take under an island, what the rule of what the staff may take keeps from a take (With no forces named, item 1) counts as well, for every other take of the same island whose plan has not ended, whatever their order, what the "ready a landing" in force under that take has: the landing's own force (the force the task is of, which the take's limit does not name where the commander named the task forces), and the task forces and the land units of its forces limit, but not the source, since two landings may sail from one base. On one morning that is the take served first, since its landing is formed in the planned state before the next is served; on a later morning a landing keeps what it has until its take's plan ends, which is when its forces come free. It is the test a force with men aboard already has (Forces with men aboard), made for the choice as well. The reason of the take that goes without names the take that has the unit or the force.
   - *A force readying a landing is not a supply run's.* The same rule of decision 0151, item 5 ("a ship serves the run or the lift that has it until it is back"), at the supply, for every take, whoever made it (differences.md, row 86). A force is no free unit of a supply pool while a "ready a landing" in force is of it, that task's plan has not ended and the force's order to prepare the landing stands (the order ends when the landing has unloaded). Once it has landed, the force is the pool's again, as the island's own lift is. So the supply of a place taken does not take the ships of a landing that waits beside it.
   - *What he named is that node's.* For a take under an island, what the rule of what the staff may take keeps from a take counts as well the task forces and the land units (not the source) named in the forces limit of every take the commander made under the same island whose plan has not ended, other than the take being served, whatever their order: the staff's takes, and his nodes that did not name it, may not take it, and the reason names the node that has it. Where two of his nodes name the same unit or force, the one served first has it and the other's reason says so. What he named comes free when its plan ends, as a landing's forces do; set again, its new plan keeps it again.
   - *The date.* Where he gave his take a date, that is its date. Where he gave none and the island has one, the staff reads the island's, as it would have given it to a take it formed. One reading gives the date a take is held to, and the take's doctrine reads it wherever it read the take's own.
   - *The risk, the knowledge asked and the area* are read up the tree, as they are. *The places he did not write* are the staff's: the island forms their takes as it does, on the first morning its plan is released. *Everything beneath a node,* loading, the roster's training and rehearsal, routes, berths, the day it sails, the escort, the supply that follows, the runs and the commitments, is the staff's.
5. **The order of service is the contract's.** Takes are served by rank, then by number. His are set before the staff forms its own, so with no rank his are served first, in the order he wrote them. What a node of his names is kept for it (item 4); what no node of his names goes to the take served first that chooses it, and is that take's landing's until its plan ends. A rank he sets on a node is read first, as for any. [0232, rule 12]
6. **What the staff may and may not do to a node of his.** [0232, rule 13]

   | The staff may | The staff may not |
   | --- | --- |
   | Form beneath it the tasks its doctrine forms, and set limits on those | Withdraw it (the engine refuses: "Not yours") |
   | Choose inside what he left blank | Set, change or clear a limit on it (refused: "Not yours"), or use a unit or force outside what he named |
   | Approve its plan on the leaves he gave, and mark it complete when its place is the side's | Change its place or its date, cancel its plan or mark it failed |

**The need to know.** A take-a-place whose plan is preparing needs a count of the enemy at the place, by its knowledge limit where one is set on it or above it, and otherwise by its risk, each read up the tree (decision 0147, items 13 to 20, and decision 0159, item 12, `crates/sim-staff/src/look.rs`). The count asked for: at preserve the force an estimate of the place *seen from the air* or *by men in contact* (a *told* opening estimate never counts), dated no more than 3 days before the morning (the 3 days are a placeholder); at balanced such an estimate of any day; at press anything or nothing, and none is ever sent for. A knowledge limit asks as its setting says: a recent count as preserve does, with its own days; a count as balanced does; none as press does. Everything below that turns on what is asked turns on the setting where one is set, and the risk still sets the men wanted and the check of the men the ships carry at preserve the force (item 4).

1. **Who is sent.** On each morning the need is unmet and the side has no man of a fighting unit ashore in the place's hex, the staff sets one air group to the reconnaissance mission for the place (an order to the group, serving the end), where none the end may use is set so already: the lowest-numbered of the air groups the end's forces limit names, read up the tree, that the side may use, that the commander does not hold, that no cover, raid or strike task served earlier in the morning has taken, that can search (not a fighter, a search range above 0) and whose search range reaches the place from where it is based. Only the groups the limits name are read, never the groups aboard task forces they name. The order is given after the landing task is served, so on the forming morning the landing order comes first. The staff does this too where it could not form the landing for want of ships to carry the men wanted (below), so that the count can come. [0147 items 14 and 15]
2. **When the need is met** each group the end may use that is on reconnaissance of the place and that the commander does not hold is set to rest, by one order, and is the staff's to use again; but a group aboard the carrier force moved to look (Operating a carrier force, item 25) is not rested: it is its carrier task's. [0147 item 15; 0159 item 14]
3. **The choice made again.** On the first morning the staff holds a count seen from the air or by men in contact (dated the day before), while the plan is preparing and no man of a unit of the task is aboard, the units are chosen again as in Prepare, items 3 to 5, on that count. Where the choice differs the staff sets the forces limit of the "ready a landing" to it (`set_limit`), and that task orders the landing again with the new roster the same morning, at the cost the rules give a change of roster (chapter 7; the loading keeps half where a unit is added). An estimate of 0 asks for one unit, the first in the order. At preserve the force a choice that is short of the men wanted is not made: the limit is left as it is and the launch is held back (below). [0147 items 16 and 17]
4. **Not launched without it.** The landing is ready for leave and the release only when the need is met as well. Where a group is sent or flying, the day's reason says the landing waits for the count; so it does where a carrier force is moved to look and none of its groups reaches the place yet (Operating a carrier force, item 25). Where nothing can look (no group the end names that the side may use and the commander does not hold can search and reach the place, or the side's own men ashore there have given no count): where a recent count is asked (at preserve the force, or by the knowledge limit) the plan is not released, the staff hands in the notice `cannot_meet` on the first morning the landing is otherwise ready, once for the plan, and gives the reason each morning after, which says that nothing it may use can look and why; where a count is asked (at balanced, or by the limit) the plan goes, and the reason of its release says that it goes on no count, or on what it was told, or on the count it holds, with its grade and day. [0147 items 18 and 19]
5. **Reasons** give the estimate with its grade and its day, the count the risk asks for, the group sent with its range and its distance from the place, and what a new count changed in the choice. Where the knowledge limit sets what is asked they name it and the intent it is set on, and give the estimate's age in days ("150 men, seen from the air, of day 1, 2 days old"). A count held but older than asked is not met, and a group is sent again on that morning as on any morning the need is unmet; the units are chosen again only on the morning after a count is first held, and a second count of the same men changes no choice. [0147 item 20; 0159 item 12]
6. **The need as data.** On each morning a take's plan is preparing the staff works its need to know as data, from the side's own view and stored nowhere (decision 0159, item 13): the plan and the take; the place; what is asked (none, a count, or a recent count with its days) and the intent the knowledge limit is set on, or none where the risk sets it; what is held, the side's estimate with its men, grade, day and age in days on the morning, and what it could be in truth: where the count was seen from the air or by men in contact, from the grade's error range in the ground figures (`reconnaissance_error_low` and `_high`, `report_error_low` and `_high`; a report is the truth times a factor between the two), the truth lies between the count over the high end, cut to a whole man, and the count over the low end, raised to a whole man (none above where the low end is 0); none for a count that was told or where the figures give no range; who is looking after the morning's orders: where the need is met a group the commander holds on the mission, and otherwise the first group on reconnaissance of the place, with the day its order was given, or else the group the staff sends that morning, from that day, unless men of the side's own are ashore there or no count is asked; the carrier force moved to look and its hex, where its task stands under the take while the need is not met, or is formed that morning (Operating a carrier force, item 25); and whether the need is met. A plan that is released is no longer asked; a take that waits for its men as a draft (Waits for its men) has no need worked and nothing looks for it. A seat reads it in `plans` and `command_state` (The tools, below). [0159 item 13]

**Told to wait.** A take is not released while a limit "not before", "wait for" or "after" read up its tree is unmet (The three limits that tell a plan to wait, above; decision 0159, items 9 to 11; `crates/sim-staff/src/told.rs`). The staff reads them on each morning the landing is ready, the plan is preparing and the need to know is met (above), after the check that the place can be held (below) and before leave to launch is asked. [0159 items 9 to 11]

1. **Unmet.** "Not before" a day, on a morning before that day. "Wait for" a task force, until the side may use it (A unit before its day, above) and it lies stopped at the source the end's forces limit names, as it binds (With no forces named, below); a land unit, until the side may use it and it has men ashore in the source's hex. "After" another end, until, on a morning, its place is the side's (for an island, every place of it), read from the side's own view as Met reads it. [0159 item 9]
2. **Held.** While any is unmet the staff has readied the landing as it does with none (Prepare, Ready, The need to know) and does not release the plan: it stays preparing, leave to launch is not asked and no notice `waits_for_leave_to_launch` is handed in, and the morning's reason names each limit waited for, the intent it is set on where that is not the take, and what it waits for, with the day where there is one ("not to go before day 6"; "task_force:2 may be used from day 5"; "the place of intent:3 at (0,6) is not yet the side's"). On the first morning every one is met the take goes on as Leave to launch says. [0159 item 10]
3. **Never.** A limit that can never be met as it stands, an "after" naming an intent that is withdrawn and whose place is not the side's, or a "wait for" naming a task force the side no longer has (or one with no ship) or a land unit with no man ashore or aboard, is said once: the staff hands in the notice `cannot_meet`, once for the plan, its reason naming the limit, and gives the reason each morning after. The plan stays preparing and is not released until the commander clears or changes the limit. [0159 item 11]

**Leave to launch.** On each morning its landing is ready, the need to know is met (above), no limit tells it to wait (Told to wait, above) and the plan is preparing, the staff releases the plan (`approve`) where leave to launch is given; where it is withheld it gives no order and hands in the notice `waits_for_leave_to_launch`, with a reason that says the landing is ready and when, with leave on a morning, it would sail and land. Before the landing is ready the day's reason gives the loading, each unit's rehearsal and the morning it expects the landing to be ready, and no such notice is handed in. [0142 item 15]

**The check that it can be held.** Where a hold of a base in the place's hex is in force with a source in the forces limit on its chain (Holding a place, above), the staff works the check on each morning the landing is ready and the plan is preparing, before leave is asked or the plan released. The daily need is the daily charge of the men chosen. The days until supply follows are, in whole days: the days to land the men (the men's weight, `handling_t_per_man` a man, in the fullest ship, at the place's rate for a ship, the berth rate where its port has a berth and the lighter rate where it has none, rounded up to days and one at least), the passage back to the hold's source, one day to load, and the passage out, each passage at the cruise speed of the transport force's slowest ship, rounded up to days and one at least. The margin is the hold's, in days (10 at preserve the force, 5 at balanced, 0 at press). The supply that lands with the men is the daily need times those days and the margin, in place of the 15 days of the move below. The first run is taken to carry the daily need for 15 days. It can be held if the source's stock above its reserve is at least the supply that lands with the men and the first run together, and the room of the transport force (its ships' cargo capacity less any fuel carried as cargo) is at least each. The reason of the release, or of the morning it waits for leave, gives the check: the need, the days, the margin, the supply, the source's stock and the first run. Where it cannot be held the reason says what is short and by how much; at preserve the force (the end's risk) the staff does not ask leave and does not release: it hands in the notice `cannot_meet` once and gives the reason each morning the check fails; at balanced and at press it goes and says so. With no such hold nothing follows the landing, no check is made and the supply is the 15 days. [0144 items 10 to 17; the ruling on decision 0142's reading 8]

**Move.** Each morning the plan is released and no man of the side is ashore at the place, the staff takes one step, the first not yet done:

1. **The supply.** The force is to have aboard the daily charge of the men chosen (their men, times `daily_supply_t_per_man`, times each unit's weight factor) for 15 days, the interval of decision 0107, or for the days of the check that the place can be held where it is made (above); cut to what is aboard plus the source's stock above its reserve (none where the force does not lie at the source) and to what is aboard plus the ships' room. What is aboard counts toward it. Where more is to be taken the force is ordered to load supply to that weight (`load_to`); while that order stands and the load is not done it gives no order. With nothing to take it goes on to the next step the same morning. [0142 item 16]
2. **The men.** The lowest-numbered unit chosen with men ashore is set to attack, where it is not on attack, and the force is ordered to load it with no cargo; while that order stands and the unit still has men ashore it gives no order. A unit aboard already needs neither. [0142 item 17]
3. **The passage.** With every unit aboard and nothing more to take, the force is given a route from where it lies to the place and on to the source, marked to act at the place, and the unload order. The route and the area are as for a run: inside the intent's area read up the tree, where one is set. Each ship must have the fuel for the miles out and back at its class's rate (its fuel capacity over its endurance), as for a run. An escort is given before the route as for a run (Escorting and covering a run, above), with the place as the destination. A ship without the fuel, no route, or a run held for want of an escort stops it, and the reason says which. [0142 items 18 and 19]

So a force released on the morning of day R sails on day R plus the days to embark (one for the supply where there is any to take, one for each unit ashore) and is due on that day plus the days of the passage (its miles out at the cruise speed of its slowest ship, rounded up to whole days, at least one) less one. Once the force's route acts at the place, or it is landing there, it is given nothing more, and the "ready a landing" under the end is given nothing either, nor from the first morning a unit chosen has men ashore at the place: the landing spends what it made ready. [0142 items 18 and 20]

**A lift turned back.** Decision 0191, as amended on review and in the build (`crates/sim-staff/src/turn_back.rs`). A contact reported as another kind makes none, whatever it is in truth. The 8 hexes are this rule's and not the supply run's threat of 15 hexes, which is read at a destination. [0191 items 2, 17 and 18]

9. **Held.** A lift the commander holds is given nothing: on a morning the rule would turn it back or keep it back, the reason says it is held. [0191 item 10]

**Ashore.** On each morning the plan is released, the place is not taken and a unit chosen (one in the forces limit of the "ready a landing" under the end, a later lift's included) has men ashore at the place, the staff judges:

4. **The men wanted** are the side's estimate of the enemy's men at the place times 3, 2 or 1 by the risk, as for the choice, while more is coming to the place; with nothing more coming they are fewer (items 7 and 8). [0143 item 4; 0193 items 2 and 3]

With no estimate the reason says it attacks with no count of the enemy. With nothing to order, one reason says so.
6. **Wait.** Otherwise, except where item 9 holds them in, each unit of the operation on attack is set to defend, and nothing else is ordered of the men; with none on attack, one reason. The engine's own rule sends a spent unit back to the defence, and it is then judged again. At preserve the force the units on attack are set to defend as in item 6. [0193 items 12 and 13]

Its standing interrupt fires as its table says: once for the plan, on the first such morning. At preserve the force, where units on attack are set to defend, the notice rests on a reason of its own. A commander may then set press, send more, or withdraw the intent. [0193 items 6 and 11]

With fewer than 3 days left (the staff's figure, a placeholder) the staff begins no attack ashore, because an attack begun so late only costs men: [0194, 2 and 7 to 9]

**A second lift.** The units left behind are the fighting units of the end's forces limit not chosen, that the side may use, that the commander does not hold, and whose men are all ashore at the source. On a morning of the judgement where the place is not taken, the transport force lies stopped at the source with nothing aboard and no estimate is held or the fit men of the units that can attack are fewer than the men wanted, the staff takes the units left behind in the order of the choice (the higher assault skill, the higher experience, the lower number), each by the force's ships in number order until their berths hold its men, until the fit men that can attack and the men taken are at least the men wanted or no ship is left; with no estimate, every unit the ships can carry. It adds them to the forces limit of the "ready a landing" by `set_limit` that morning, so the choice is made once and the commander may change it; at balanced, where the lift is short with too few in reach and its men cannot be fed, it adds nothing and orders nothing (Not to starve, above). The lift then goes one step a morning as the first did (Move, above): supply for its men for 15 days, a unit a morning by a load order, then the route and the unload order with an escort as for a run. A unit of a second lift is not set to attack before it sails, and the force goes as it stands, with no landing made ready, and the reason says so. Nothing more is sent once the place is taken or no unit is left behind; where the men are short and none is left, the reason says so, and the staff works whether more is coming and acts on it (Ashore, items 7 to 10). [0143 items 9 to 14; 0193 item 10]

**Forces with men aboard.** A **lift as it stands** is a transport force the staff may take (With no forces named, item 1) with men aboard a ship afloat. Its units are the units aboard it, whole; its source, for its way back and for the check that the place can be held, is its home port where that is the side's, else the nearest base of the side by the engine's sea path, the lower number on a tie. It is taken only when it is free: it has no route left to follow and is not landing, so a force under way on an order keeps it until it stops. [0152 item 1; the architect's ruling of Nov 18, 2026]

1. **The first lift.** With no forces named, the lifts as they stand with a fighting unit the staff may take aboard come before any base: the first lift is the lowest-numbered such force that is free, is not the lift of another take whose plan has not ended and was not sent by another intent that morning, and the base rule (item 3) is used only where there is none. The units chosen are its fighting units aboard, whole, and the men counted against the men wanted are theirs; the working units aboard land with them, are not units of the operation for the judgement Ashore, and the reason names them. [0152 items 2 and 3]
2. **Everyone aboard lands,** and the supply is reckoned for every man aboard. A lift that does not lie at its source, or whose source the staff may not take (decision 0149, item 5), takes nothing more: it goes with what is aboard, and the reason gives what it is short. A lift as it stands is ready as it stands (Ready, above). [0152 item 4]
3. **Later lifts.** On a morning the plan is released and the place is not taken, once the first lift is no longer on its way there (it is not landing there and the place is not ahead on its route, or it is lost), and no estimate is held or the fit men of the units that can attack are fewer than the men wanted (with no man of the operation ashore, none), the staff sends each other lift as it stands with a fighting unit aboard that is free, that no other take has as its lift, that no intent sent that morning and that is inside what the commander or the island named, in force order, on the first morning the side may use it, save at balanced one short of the men wanted with too few in reach whose men cannot be fed (Not to starve, above), which is not sent that morning; then the units left behind, as A second lift has it. Each later lift is given a route from where it lies to the place and on to its source, marked to act at the place, and the unload order, that morning: it has nothing to load. Its force and its fighting units are added to the forces limit of the "ready a landing" by `set_limit`, the force too, since its orders are checked against that limit. Its units are not set to attack before they sail. Each lift is its own force and goes when it can. While the first lift is on its way the later lifts wait, so each take in priority order is served on the first morning with one lift. On a morning the takes are served in priority order for every lift as it stands: the later lifts a take higher in priority sends that morning are set aside before any take lower in priority chooses its first lift, and a lower take forms nothing from them; its reason names each as a later lift of the higher take. A first lift with no ship afloat is lost, whether or not the engine still lists its force, and so is no longer on its way. The reason of the judgement gives each force with men aboard and why it is or is not sent. [0152 items 5 to 7, 5a and 5b]
4. **By night.** A later lift goes by night as a lift does (A run by night). [0152 item 9]
5. **Where a lift goes when it has landed.** A lift of "take a place", the first or a later one, whether as it stands or made at a base, and a force a hold sends (Holding a place, Forces with men aboard) is routed to the place and on to the source of the hold of that place, where a hold of a base in the place's hex is in force with a source in the forces limit on its chain (the lowest-numbered such hold) and every ship of the force has the fuel for the passage to the place and on to that source, by the engine's measure at the speed it goes (by night, the speed the night's choice gives, else cruising speed; by day, its own). Else it is routed back to its own source, where it came from, as before; where the two are one base nothing changes. The reason of the route names the base it goes on to and why, with each ship's fuel against the burn to the place and the burn on; where it goes back for want of fuel it gives the same figures. The source the island names on a morning is read by the take and the hold of that same morning, since the island's tree is formed first. Once it lies at the source with nothing aboard it joins the hold's pool (Controlling an island, The source kept, and its pool); a force with men left aboard or supply it did not land does not. Nothing else is done to it: a take whose first lift has gone on to another base than its own source makes no second lift with it from there. [0163 items 4 to 6]

**Reasons** carry the numbers in the order a staff works in: the estimate and the men wanted; each unit in its order with its assault skill, its experience and its men; the berths and the ships; each unit left out and why; the supply in tons; the days to embark and of the passage. **A proposal** shows the task formed, its units, ships, supply and days, and goes no further. [0142 items 27 and 28]

A side sees: its own orders, reasons and notices, whole. The enemy sees none. [0142]

## The plan order

Decision 0159, rule E (items 15 to 17), step 6 of the opening of stage 4, widened by decision 0164. For each intent of the commander's in force, and for each "take a place" in force that the staff formed (under a "control an island", say), the staff hands in a **plan order**: what it already works out of the plan, as data. It is worked when asked, from the side's own view and command state and the staff's own sums of that morning, and stored nowhere: it is not state, is not hashed or saved, and changes no order the staff gives. Asked at the start of a day it is the morning of that day's staff step, before that morning's decisions: the plan's state, its phases and its tasks are read from the command state as the morning finds it; what it waits for, the men, the day ashore and the need to know are the staff's working of that morning. [0159 items 15 and 16]

It has:

1. **The intent and its mission,** the intent in words as the staff's reasons state it; **the plan** and its state.
2. **The phases,** in order, each with the condition that ends it and whether it is done, under way or to come. A take's: **prepare**, ended by the release; **move**, ended by the first man of the side ashore at the place; **land**, ended when the place is the side's. A draft has all three to come; a plan preparing has prepare under way; released, move is under way until a man is ashore and land then, and all three are done once the place is the side's or the plan is complete. A hold of a base that is not the side's: **wait**, ended when the base is the side's, then **hold**, which does not end; a hold of the side's own base has hold alone. A hold's draft has its phases to come. Every other kind has no phases. [0159 item 16]
3. **What it waits for:** the things that must be so before a take's plan is released, while it is a draft or preparing, each with its kind, whether it is met and the day it is expected to be met where the staff can work one. Only what the staff waits on is listed, in this order: **men** (Waits for its men, above: the men wanted and to hand, expected on the day that brings them, with none where no day in reach does); **knowledge** (the count asked, where a count is asked and the staff holds the landing for it: met, or not met with a group looking, a force moved to look or a recent count asked; never with a day); the limits **a day** ("not before", expected that day), **a force** ("wait for" a task force or a land unit, expected on the day the side may use it where it lies at the source, else with no day) and **an intent** ("after", with no day), met or not, as Told to wait reads them; **leave** to prepare (of a draft) or to launch (of a plan preparing), where the commander withholds it, with no day. A count asked where nothing can look and the take goes all the same is not listed, nor a leave that is given. Once the plan is released it waits for nothing. [0159 item 15]
4. **The men wanted and to hand,** where the side holds a count of the enemy at the place: the count times 3, 2 or 1 by the risk (Prepare, item 5), against the men ashore and aboard of the units of the take's "ready a landing", or, before it is formed, the men to hand that the wait for men found. With no count, none. [0159 item 15]
5. **The day it expects to be ashore,** by the sums a take uses for a latest day: the morning the landing would be ready (Ready, and the projection of Readying a landing; for a take that waits for its men, a landing begun on the day that brings them, with that day's lift), or the latest day a thing waited for is expected where that is later, plus the days to embark and of the passage (Move), less 1. A released plan counts from the morning asked, with the days to embark it then has. None where a thing waited for has no day, where men of the side are ashore at the place or the place is the side's.
6. **The need to know,** for a take whose plan is preparing, as The need to know, item 6, gives it.
7. **The tasks:** the intents in force under it, each with its kind, its maker and its phase: a "ready a landing" under a take is the prepare phase's, a "bombard a base" the move phase's, any other the phase under way. Since decision 0232 the order of an island lists every intent in force beneath it, the commander's takes among them, where it listed those the staff formed; its text says "Yours:" before his takes and "The staff's:" before the rest. [0232, rule 18]

A day on which a task had no order is not in the plan order: it stays in the task's reasons. [0159 item 16]

The plan order of a take the staff formed is worked exactly as that of a commander's take, items 1 to 7: it has its own phases, what it waits for, its men, its day ashore, its need to know and its tasks. No other intent of the staff's has a plan order. The orders are given in the number order of their intents, the commander's and the staff's takes together. The order of the commander's own intent is unchanged by it, and still lists the take among its tasks. A take the commander wrote has a plan order of its own, as every intent of the commander's has, and so has a staff's take. [0164 items 1, 2 and 4; 0232, rule 18]

The tool `order` gives it (The tools, below) and the table's plan sheet shows it. It tells a side nothing it does not already have. [0159 item 17]
