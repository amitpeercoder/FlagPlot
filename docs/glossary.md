# Glossary

One meaning for each word the rules use. The complexity review after scenario 5 found these used in more than one sense. Decision records, event texts and briefings follow this page; where an older record says otherwise, this page is right.

| Word | Means | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Air distance** | How far an aircraft flies between two hexes: the straight line between their centres, in whole miles (decision 0040). Every range, radius and sortie distance, and every tool that measures one, uses it | The number of hexes between them times 40, which is longer off the grid's axes |
| **Sea distance** | How far a ship is charged for steaming between two hexes by the map's shortest sea path: the sum of the charges of its steps. Where the way is clear it is the air distance; where land forces a detour the steps are charged 40 miles until the way is clear (decision 0058) | The hex count of the path times 40 miles |
| **Step charge** | What the step from one hex centre to the next is charged, in miles, 1 to 40: the miles it brings the force closer to its waypoint in a straight line where the way is clear, 40 where it is not (decision 0058) | |
| **Clear way** | From a hex, a sea path to the waypoint with as many steps as the hex distance: no land forces a detour (decision 0058) | |
| **Readiness** | What a force is ordered to be: normal or battle (decision 0021) | Whether it is alert at this moment |
| **Alert** | The state of a force whose lookouts see at full effect. A force at battle readiness is always alert | An order |
| **Ready** | A force's state as a fight begins, kept for the whole fight: at battle readiness, or alert since before the present pulse began. A force that is not ready fires its guns at a share, `unready_gun_share`, in every round, and its torpedoes at a share, `unready_torpedo_share` (decisions 0068, 0071) | Alert. A force alerted in this pulse sees at full effect and is not ready |
| **Alerted** | How a force at normal readiness came to be alert: by a fresh contact nearby, by being fired on, or by an alarm. A contact never alerts or stands down a force at battle readiness | |
| **Alarm** | The roll a force that is not alert makes when a fight with gunfire happens in its hex | |
| **Stance** | A force's ordered way of meeting an enemy: engage or avoid (decision 0014) | |
| **Avoiding for the pulse** | What a force does after it breaks off or slips away: it takes the avoid stance until the pulse ends, whatever its orders (decision 0022) | A change to its orders |
| **Break off** | To end a fight because the share of a force's ships sunk or heavily damaged has reached its break-off level, or because it is avoiding and was fired on | To move. A force that breaks off keeps to its route |
| **Slip away** | To end a fight unseen: an avoiding force that has seen an enemy that has not seen it | |
| **Keep share** | The share of its fit ships a force must keep when it detaches stragglers (decision 0016) | |
| **Patrol share** | The share of its aircraft a fighter group keeps over its own hex (decision 0027) | |
| **Sight** | What an air search does to a force: it sights it, and a report follows | |
| **See** | What a force does in a fight, round by round | |
| **Report** | What a side is told. It can be wrong. Every change to what a side believes is a report | The truth |
| **Contact** | What a side believes about an enemy force: a hex, a count, a kind and an age. Made and refreshed by reports. Each report gives a hex, a count and a kind, any of which can be wrong; a later report of the same contact replaces them, so its kind and count can change from report to report | The force itself |
| **Position** | A force's hex: the nearest hex centre. A group aboard a ship is at its ship's force's position | How far along a leg it is, which is working data of movement |
| **Radius** | How far out an aircraft type goes and comes back. `search_range_nm` is the largest range a search arc may name, and the engine refuses an arc beyond it; it is the search radius, derived on its own from the range in the scouting condition (decision 0075). `strike_range_nm` is what a group's reach is measured from. Both columns are named range and hold radii | Range |
| **Reach** | How far an air group's strikes and escorts may go: normal, the type's strike radius, or extended, the radius and a share more. Set by order, normal until then (decision 0059) | Radius |
| **Extended reach** | The reach of a group that has been ordered to it: the radius times (1 plus the extended reach share), cut to a whole mile. A sortie beyond the radius may cost aircraft that run out of fuel on the way home | Normal reach |
| **Target list** | The kinds of ship a strike group goes for, in order, set by order. The default is all six kinds in the order of importance. A group is launched only at a contact taken for a kind on its list, and attacks over the target the ship its list ranks first, the kinds not on it following in the order of importance (decision 0063) | Target priority, priorities |
| **In the air** | Describes the aircraft of a group that flew in the pulse and were not shot down, until the wear phase of the pulse brings them home (decision 0059) | Aboard |
| **Landed elsewhere** | Describes aircraft that were in the air when their own deck was lost and landed at another carrier or a base, joining or forming a group there (decision 0059) | Rebased |
| **Rebase** | An order that moves an air group at a base to another base of its side at once. Its ready aircraft and pilots go with it, its damaged aircraft are written off, and it flies nothing that day (decision 0091) | Landed elsewhere; a group moving with its ship |
| **Transfer range** | How far an air group may be rebased: twice the longer of its type's search range and strike radius, by air distance between the two bases (decision 0091) | Reach; radius |
| **Ditched** | Describes an aircraft that ran out of fuel on the way home from a sortie beyond its radius, or that found no deck to land on, and came down in the sea. It is gone from the group for good and its crew may be recovered or lost (decision 0059) | Shot down, written off |
| **Range** | How far an aircraft flies one way, as a sourced fact. Only the derivation tool reads it. The engine reads the radius columns, whose names say range (corrected against the code, which reads `search_range_nm`) | |
| **Crippled** | A ship afloat, at sea, in port or at the yard, whose propulsion or flight deck is out. The objective `ships_crippled` counts them; degraded does not count (decision 0054) | Heavily damaged; slowed |
| **Heavily damaged** | A ship with flotation damage of `surface_combat.toml: crippled_flotation` (50) or more. It counts toward its force's break-off level and has no other effect. The figure, the break-off event's text and its breakdown factor still say crippled | Crippled |
| **Straggler** | A ship whose propulsion is degraded or out. The straggler instruction acts on it | Heavily damaged |
| **Degraded, out** | The two damaged states of a named system. Degraded halves what the system does; out stops it | |
| **Raid** | The strikes launched in one pulse from one place at one hex, with their escorts | One air group's strike |
| **Mission** | What an air group is ordered to fly: rest, search, naval strike, combat air patrol. A task force also has a mission, which no rule reads yet | |
| **Placeholder** | A figure invented so that the engine runs. Never historical | |
| **Settled** | A choice between sourced figures, made by the architect and written down with its reason | Stated by a source |
| **Pulse** | One of the three parts of a game day, resolved in this order: night, morning, afternoon. A pulse is the same across the whole map (decision 0001). Air searches and strikes fly only in the morning and afternoon: the night pulse is dark, its search phase is empty and no strike is launched in it | Darkness. The night pulse is dark, and light is asked of one function |
| **Phase** | A named step the day runs in a fixed order, inside a pulse or after the last pulse. Some phases are empty slots | A pulse |
| **Draw** | One random number taken from a stream keyed on the seed, day, pulse, subsystem, entity and rule. A chance succeeds if its draw is below it (decision 0003) | A number from a generator that advances as the game runs |
| **Hash** | The 64-bit summary of the state at the end of a day. Two runs agree if their daily hashes agree (decision 0004) | A security measure |
| **Saved game** | A file holding the scenario's name and hash, the hash of each data file, the seed, who held each seat, the orders as handed in and the daily hashes. It holds no state (decision 0043) | A snapshot a game can be resumed from |
| **Replay** | Running a saved game again from the start with no player, and failing on the first day whose hash differs | The replay file the viewer reads, which is an exported run |
| **Seat** | A side's place in a game. A person, an agent or the script holds it (decision 0050) | A unit |
| **Unit** | A task force or an air group: what an order is addressed to | A ship |
| **Issuer** | Who gave an order: the player, the staff or the local commander. It is part of the order's origin | Who holds the seat |
| **Origin** | Who issued an order and the intent it serves. Every order carries one (decision 0008) | |
| **Staff** | The planner that gives orders between days. It is not built | The local commander |
| **Local commander** | A rule that gives an order for a unit on the spot, inside a day. Today it is the straggler rule's order to head for the home port (decisions 0016 and 0055) | The staff |
| **Intake** | The step at the start of a day that numbers, checks and applies or refuses the orders waiting (decision 0008) | |
| **Standing order** | An accepted order that stays in force until another order of its slot replaces it | An order carried out and finished |
| **Cause** | An order or an earlier event that an event names as what led to it (decision 0047 cuts them for a side) | |
| **Breakdown** | The factors of a computed quantity or chance, recorded when recording is on. It never changes what happens or the hash. A side is shown a whole event's breakdown whole, less any raw draw; a claim's not at all (decision 0060); and of an event cut of more than an enemy's name, only the factors that are its own, with no result (decisions 0047 and 0061); an event cut of nothing but the enemy's name keeps its whole breakdown. So its own search that sighted shows the aircraft, sweep width and arc but no distance or chance; its own attack shows the aircraft but no chance; its own fighters' and guns' fire show the shots but no chance; its own salvo shows its guns or tubes, rate and its own factors but no chance. The side fired on is given no breakdown | |
| **Whole, cut, absent** | How a side is given an event: as the log has it, cut down to what the side may know, or not at all (decision 0047). A cut event never names an enemy ship, task force or air group (decision 0065) | |
| **Contact lost** | How a side is told a fight ended when its own force did not end it: the enemy's force sank, or broke off. The enemy's losses reach the side as its claim (decision 0085) | That the enemy got away |
| **Hidden causes** | The flag on a side's event that says it may have causes the side cannot see. It is set for every event of a kind that can have them, so it says nothing of what the enemy did (decision 0085) | That this event had a cause that was dropped |
| **Handle** | How a side names a unit to the game and is shown it: its kind and a number, `task_force:3`. A side's own ships, task forces, air groups and land units are numbered 1, 2, 3 among its own; a base and a contact keep their numbers (decisions 0086, 0066) | The unit's number in the scenario file, which the umpire's tools show |
| **Hex kind** | One of deep sea, shallow sea, coast and land | |
| **Navigable** | Describes a hex a ship may enter: deep sea, shallow sea or coast that is on the map | Land |
| **Closed side** | A side of a hex a ship cannot cross. A mark on either hex closes it | |
| **Route** | The list of waypoints a force steams to, with the index of the next one | The path between them, which is never stored |
| **Waypoint** | A hex a route names. A force reaches it when it reaches the hex's centre | |
| **Leg** | The step between the centres of two neighbouring hexes, charged `leg_nm` miles (1 to 40), set when it begins. Once begun it is always finished at that charge | |
| **Segment** | A force's steaming from the start of a pulse or its last fight to its next fight or the end of the pulse. It is recorded as one steamed event | A leg |
| **Budget** | The miles a force may steam in a pulse: the lower of its time limit and its fuel limit | |
| **Fuel limit** | How far the fuel of a force's least-fuelled ship lasts at the force's speed | The ship's fuel capacity |
| **Burn** | The fuel a ship uses steaming a distance: the distance times the cruise burn per mile, times the speed multiplier (decision 0006) | Fuel carried as cargo, which cannot be burned |
| **Under way** | The status of a force that has a route to steam. A force dead in the water is still under way | Moving |
| **Arrived** | The status of a force that has reached the centre of its last waypoint | Docked |
| **Mark, act at** | A hex a route order names, one of the route's waypoints, where the force is to act. Every waypoint at a marked hex is marked, and the last waypoint is never treated as marked. A mark halts a force only if it has work there (decision 0096) | A waypoint that the force always stops at |
| **Halt, halted** | A force's stop at a marked waypoint where it has work, a bombardment or cargo. The status is halted: at the hex centre, not moving, stopped as an arrived force is. It lasts until its work is done (decision 0096) | Arrived |
| **Steam on** | What a halted force does when its work is done: it resumes its route toward the waypoint after the one it halted at, in the same pulse and with the pulse's full hours (decision 0096) | Retiring |
| **Out of fuel** | The status of a force whose fuel ran out before its last waypoint. It takes no route, load or load-fuel order until it has taken fuel in port | A force with no fuel that is not under way |
| **Stopped** | Not under way: idle, arrived, halted or out of fuel. Refuelling and cargo handling also ask that the force is exactly at a hex centre | |
| **Dead in the water** | Describes a ship whose propulsion is out, or the force it slows. Its speed is 0. Such a ship is also crippled | Heavily damaged |
| **Sound ship** | A ship whose propulsion is working | A ship with no damage |
| **Cargo** | Supply or fuel carried in a ship's hold, in tons, up to the class's cargo capacity. Men are not cargo room, but they are worked as cargo, at a weight per man (decision 0066) | The fuel a ship steams on; the men aboard |
| **Stock** | The tons of supply or of fuel a base holds | |
| **Berth** | A place alongside at a base where a ship works cargo at the berth rate. A size 0 port has none (decision 0011) | |
| **Lighter** | The boats that work cargo for a ship with no berth, at the lighter rate | |
| **Land unit** | A count of men with a side and a name, a stable number, a posture, fatigue, a fed share, a march (or none), a rehearsal, an activity, experience and days counted. Its men are ashore in one hex, aboard ships, or part each. It is destroyed when it has no men ashore or aboard, and keeps its number (decisions 0066, 0090, 0094, 0095, 0097) | A ship, a task force or an air group |
| **Troop capacity** | A ship class's `troop_capacity_men`: the men a ship can carry, apart from her cargo capacity. Men take no cargo room and cargo takes no berths (decision 0066) | Cargo capacity |
| **Men ashore, men aboard** | A land unit's men in a hex, and its men in ships, each ship holding men of one unit only. Men aboard do not eat and are lost with a ship that sinks (decision 0066) | |
| **Weight of a man** | `ground.toml: handling_t_per_man`: the tons of a ship's work in a pulse that moving one man takes. Men are worked as cargo at it, first, in whole men (decision 0066) | The tons a man weighs |
| **Load order with a unit** | The load order with a land unit beside its cargo, which is supply, fuel or none. It takes the unit's men ashore in the force's hex aboard first, and needs no base (decision 0066) | The plain load order, which names supply or fuel and needs a base |
| **Landing** | A force's unload of men on a coast hex: men first, then supply and fuel. The first man landed where the side has no base forms one (decision 0066) | The unloading of cargo at a base |
| **Base formed by a landing** | A base made in play by the first man a force lands in a hex where its side has none. It has the next free base number, the port of the hex, no stock, no guns and no repairs, and is not on the enemy's map until it changes side or a ground report finds it (decisions 0066, 0090, 0092) | A base the scenario starts with, or a dump a march forms |
| **On the map** | Describes a base the scenario starts with, unless it gives `on_map = false`: its number, name, hex and holding side are told to both sides. A base formed by a landing or a march, or one the scenario holds off the map, is not on the map of the enemy, who is refused a base strike against it as against a base that does not exist, and whose forces cannot bombard it, until it changes side or a ground report finds it; a base that has changed side is on both maps with the side that holds it now (decisions 0066, 0090, 0092) | The map of hexes |
| **Land link** | A pair of neighbouring hexes, each land or coast, that a map joins by land, so that men can march between them. It runs both ways and is map data, not state. A map with no links allows no march (decision 0094) | A closed side of a hex, which is for ships |
| **March** | A land unit's order and its state: its men ashore go to a neighbouring hex a land link joins to theirs, in `ground.toml: march_days_per_hex` days. Until they arrive they are where they were. It stands until the unit arrives or another march order replaces it (decision 0094) | A ship's or a force's movement, or the posture of a unit |
| **Days marched** | The days a unit has marched toward the hex it marches to so far, 0 up to the figure less one. A day is added in the first step of the ground combat phase, and on the last the unit arrives (decision 0094) | |
| **Dump** | A base formed by a march: the name "Dump at (q,r)", no guns, no repairs, not on the enemy's map until a ground report finds it. It is formed in the hex a unit arrives in when its side has no base there and the unit carried supply (decision 0094) | A base formed by a landing, which a force forms by landing men |
| **Base found** | A base put on a side's map by a ground report: an enemy base in the hex of the report that was not on the map. It is on the map from then on, and the side may bombard it (decision 0092) | A base taken |
| **Unit divided, detachment** | A land unit's men aboard a force, made a new unit when the force is about to land them in a hex while the unit has men ashore in another. The new unit is named for the unit it came from, ", detachment" and its number. Units are never merged (decision 0066) | A task force detached by order |
| **Fed, unfed** | Of the men ashore in a hex on a night: the men fed are the tons all the bases of their side in the hex paid over the charge for a man, cut; the rest are unfed, and a share of them is lost (decisions 0066, 0090) | Men aboard, who are neither |
| **Fed share** | A land unit's number from 0 to 1: the men of its side fed in its hex at the last night's charge over the men charged there. It is 1 until the first charge and for a unit with no man ashore at it, and it cuts the unit's firing men (decision 0090) | The fatigue of a unit |
| **Posture** | A land unit's state, defend or attack, set by the scenario or by the order `set_posture`. A unit on attack in a hex with enemy men starts a fight there, and a tired unit goes back to defend (decision 0090) | A task force's stance, which is engage or avoid |
| **Contested hex** | A hex in which both sides have men ashore when the day's fights begin, after the marches, and at least one unit is on attack. It has one fight a day (decision 0090) | A hex in which two forces meet at sea |
| **Firing men** | A land unit's men ashore times its fed share times 100 less its fatigue over 100, cut to a whole man. Shots are the firing men over `ground.toml: men_per_shot` (decision 0090) | The men ashore |
| **Beaten** | Of a side in a hex: the other side attacked there that day, and the side has no man ashore or fewer than `ground.toml: beaten_share` times the other side's men, counted after the day's losses. A base with no men falls to an attacker without a fight, and the side is beaten. A beaten side loses every base it holds in the hex (decision 0090) | Sunk, or destroyed |
| **Change of side** | What a base does when its side is beaten in its hex: it keeps its number, name, hex, guns and repair figures, passes to the other side with `ground.toml: captured_stock_share` of its supply and fuel, is on both sides' maps, and the air groups and the ships in port at it are lost (decision 0090) | A base formed by a landing |
| **Loading** | A task force's number from 0 to 100 for one landing place: the ships stowed for the landing, and the boats and their crews. A force gains `ground.toml: preparation_per_day` of it for each day it spends in port with a prepare-landing order and a roster. It scales the rate at which the force unloads at that hex while it lands men. A new landing place loses all of it, a unit added to the roster some of it, and the landing at that hex spends it (decision 0095) | The load order or the loading phase, which work cargo |
| **Rehearsal** | A land unit's number from 0 to 100 for one hex: the men knowing the plan, the boats and the beach. A unit on a force's roster gains the same figure for each day it spends ready with the force in port. It cuts the fatigue the unit gains when its first men land there, and the landing spends it (decision 0095) | Training, which raises experience and lasts |
| **Roster** | The land units a task force's prepare-landing order names as those that are to land from it. A unit is on one roster at a time, and leaves it when it is dropped, when another force's order names it, or when its first men land from a ship (decision 0095) | The units a force carries |
| **Activity** | What a land unit does on a day it neither marches nor fights, set by the order `set_activity`: ready or train (work is not built). A unit does one thing a day, and the activity and the day's other facts say which (decisions 0099, 0097) | Posture, which is for the fight |
| **Training** | An activity: a unit set to train, with all its men ashore at a base of its side and fed in full, counts a day of training. Each `ground.toml: training_days_per_level` days raise its experience by one, up to `training_ceiling` (decision 0097) | Rehearsal, which is for one landing |
| **Days of fighting** | A land unit's count of days on which it fired in a fight. Each `ground.toml: fight_days_per_level` days raise its experience by one, up to 4 (decision 0097) | Days of training |
| **Group lost with its base** | An air group at a base that changed side: its aircraft and pilots go to 0 and its place is lost. It flies nothing, receives nothing, and an order to it is refused (decision 0090) | A group lost with its ship |
| **Arc** | The sector an air group searches: a from bearing, a to bearing and a range in miles | |
| **Bearing** | Degrees clockwise from north, in whole multiples of ten | |
| **Sweep width** | The width of sea one aircraft of a type sees (`aircraft_types*.csv: sweep_width_nm`) | |
| **Starting contact** | A contact a scenario gives a side at the start. It is entered as a report with the stated hex, count and kind, then aged (decision 0021) | A report with errors |
| **Fresh** | Describes a contact whose age is no more than `search.toml: fresh_contact_max_age_pulses`. A fresh contact can be refreshed by a report and can alert a force | Describes a contact that can be struck, which has its own limit |
| **Forgotten** | Describes a contact whose age has reached `search.toml: contact_drop_age_pulses`. It is dropped | |
| **Taken for** | The kind a strike's contact carried when the strike was launched. The raid's claim names it | The kind of ship that was there |
| **Claim** | A report of what a side's own people believe they did to the enemy. It can be wrong and it never ages (decisions 0014 and 0056). The claim of a shore bombardment is of aircraft destroyed on the ground and nothing else (decision 0092) | A loss in truth |
| **Ground report** | A report to a side of the enemy's men ashore in a hex: the true count times an error drawn between `ground.toml: report_error_low` and `report_error_high`, cut to a whole man. It is made each day in each hex where both sides have men ashore, after the day's fight, and it makes or replaces the side's estimate for the hex and finds the enemy's bases there (decision 0092) | A sighting report, which is of ships |
| **Estimate (of the enemy ashore)** | What a side believes of the enemy's men in one hex: a count, the day it was reported and the report's event. There is one for a hex, and a new report replaces it. It is never aged, dropped or corrected. It may be wrong (decision 0092) | A count of the truth. A contact, which is of ships |
| **Opening estimate** | An estimate a scenario gives a side at the start, with no error, as a starting contact is given. It is what that side believed, right or wrong, and is dated day 0 (decision 0092) | A starting contact |
| **Fight** | The rounds that two opposing task forces fight when they meet (decision 0014) | |
| **Meeting** | Two opposing forces in one hex, either because one entered it or because both began a pulse there and neither will move. Each meeting is one fight (decision 0022) | |
| **Round** | One step of a fight, `surface_combat.toml: round_minutes` minutes long | |
| **Salvo** | One ship's gun fire or its torpedo fire in one round at one target | |
| **Tubes on one side** | A ship class's `torpedo_tubes_one_side`: the torpedo tubes that bear on one side, from 0 to its `torpedo_tubes`. A derived class takes it from the rows (decision 0074); a centreline fit, and every placeholder and frozen class, has all its tubes on one side. A ship fires this many in her first torpedo salvo and the rest in her second (decision 0083) | `torpedo_tubes`, every tube aboard |
| **Side fired** | A ship's `torpedoes_one_side_fired`: true once she has fired the tubes of one side and before she fires the other's (decision 0083) | |
| **Radar** | A yes or no for a ship class, read by one rule: a force with a ship afloat of a class that has it adds `radar_sighting_bonus` to its chance to sight in a fight. A derived class has it only if a ship of it has a surface-search set whose display has a plan position indicator; an air-search set does not give it, and a surface-search set with no row for its display does not either (decision 0077) | Radar fire control, which no rule has; an air-search set |
| **Shot** | One unit of fire that can hit: one gun round, one torpedo tube or one aircraft's weapon. Each whole shot is one draw (decision 0033) | |
| **Hit** | A shot that succeeded | |
| **Shore bombardment** | A force on the bombardment mission, in the night pulse, firing its ships' guns at an enemy base in its hex that is on its side's map. The shells hit aircraft on the ground, supply and men ashore, or nothing. Nothing ashore fires back (decision 0092) | A fight between forces, or an air raid on a base |
| **Weighed shot** | A bombardment shell counted by its gun: the shells fired times the class's `gun_damage` over `surface_combat.toml: bombardment_reference_gun_damage`. A heavy shell counts for several, a light one for a part of one. Each is rolled at `bombardment_hit_chance` (decision 0092) | A shell fired |
| **Men lost to fire** | Men a bomb or a shell hit on men ashore removed from a land unit: `ground.toml: men_lost_per_base_hit` for each hit, no more than are there. Told to the unit's own side and never to the side that fired (decision 0092) | Men lost in a fight, unfed or with a ship |
| **Damage points** | What one hit adds to a ship's track: 100 times the weapon's damage over the target class's toughness | |
| **Damage track** | One of a ship's two damage numbers, flotation and fire, each from 0 to 100 | |
| **System hit** | A hit that also steps one of a ship's named systems down a level (decisions 0016 and 0033) | |
| **Forward repair** | The staff's mending of ship damage at a base that has `ship_repair_points_per_day`, at the start of each day after the orders, for the forces of its side stopped there, with no order. It mends fire, then flotation below `forward_repair_max_flotation`, then degraded systems, and never a system that is out (decision 0069) | The yard; repair of aircraft; repair of a ship lying in port, which is not built |
| **Bank** | A ship's `system_repair_points`: the points forward repair has put into her first degraded system and not finished, from 0 to below `forward_repair_system_points` (decision 0069) | The base's allowance for the day |
| **Port for the yard** | A base with a `yard_passage_days`. A force stopped at one can be sent to the yard (decision 0069) | Any base |
| **Days of repair** | One ship's days at the yard from her damage alone, no passage: the days for her flotation damage, her degraded systems and her systems out, rounded up to a whole day. Fire adds nothing (decision 0069) | The days away |
| **Days away** | A force's days at the yard: the port's `yard_passage_days` plus the largest days of repair among its ships afloat (decision 0069) | One ship's days of repair |
| **Day of return** | The day a force sent to the yard is back: the day the order is taken in plus the days away. It is back at the start of that day (decision 0069) | The last day away |
