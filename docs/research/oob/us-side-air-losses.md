Two limits. September runs to the 30th, thirteen days past the period. The Army's P-400s are not in it: four were shot down on 30 August and a few more were lost on the ground or in accidents, from other sources.

**Totals for the two months, Navy and Marine (the sums are mine):** 25 lost to enemy aircraft, 3 to anti-aircraft fire, 5 operational in action, 18 on other flights, **2 on the ground**. Credited: 163 enemy aircraft.

## By day, from the dispatches

The Command Summary carries the commanders' own daily figures for thirteen days of the period (rows 1321 to 1361). Examples: 24 August, 3 fighters lost and 10 bombers claimed; 2 September, one dive bomber destroyed on the ground and none lost in the air; 10 September, one fighter missing and four bombers claimed, with 11 fighters, 22 dive bombers and 3 P-400s flyable; 13 September, four fighters lost. It has nothing for 26 to 30 August.

**The field.** One day comes close to a closure: on 9 September the field was "practically inoperative", after nine aircraft crashed using it on the 8th. A dispatch of 11 September says rain "has reduced efficiency Cactus field to point where several planes crashed in take off". Shortages of steel mat and of fuel recur. No dispatch reports the field shut by bombing or shelling.

| Japanese aircrew against US aircraft on the ground | 60 to 80 | 2 Navy and Marine, and one or two P-400s | 15 to 1 or more | The same |

**For the claim chances of decision 0055:** both sides' fighters overclaimed aircraft in the air by about three to one, with no clear difference between them on this evidence. Bomber crews' claims of aircraft destroyed on the ground were worth almost nothing. The ratios are my arithmetic on the rows.

## Where sources disagree

- 8 September: nine aircraft crashed on the field by the dispatch, against five, four and one in rows already in the data.
- 10 September: four bombers claimed by the dispatch, against five.
- 24 and 25 August: the dispatch and the running estimate give different US fighter losses, and it is not clear they describe the same raid.
