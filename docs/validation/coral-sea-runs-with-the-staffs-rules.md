1. **Run 2, the lift turned back,** brought the scenario inside the band by itself: the convoy lands whole in 520 games and not at all in 476, and almost never in part. It is two games and not one close one: which it is turns on whether the convoy has been hurt when a carrier is sighted near its way.
2. **Run 3 makes the convoy a target.** A transport is sunk in 727 games where it was sunk in 48 and in 12. The convoy lands in part in 314 games: the result is now spread across every level, with all seven reached in numbers.
3. **The carriers' strikes go to the convoy and leave the Striking Force:** Shokaku is not sunk in any game of run 3. Shoho is sunk as often as before.
4. **The support group pays for its station:** Australia is sunk in 150 games against 51. It lies in the waters Rabaul's bombers deny.
5. **Lexington is never sunk, in any run.** Not looked into.
