## Why nothing moves

The best that supported moves could reach is about 5 to 6 lost to the enemy.

- **Bombers' return fire** could carry most of the patrol's missing losses over the carriers. It is a new rule, deferred since decision 0027, and no row counts the fighters the gunners shot down.
- **The protection factors** are the one figure in the escorts' fire. The carrier fight's exchange, 6 of the 10 escorts lost (291) against about 6 American fighters, points toward parity. But no source sizes armour or tanks, they are read in every fight, and at Rabaul American fighters lost already sits at its ceiling of 23. Moving them would fit one battle with a figure both share, which tuning rule 7 warns against.
- **The rest are rule gaps or a scenario order:** the search fights, the late group, the write-off for damage, the fuel spent while a deck was closed.

The American claims measure divides by these losses' Japanese counterpart, not by them, and is the claims round's.
