You command the Allied side in one game of Flag Plot, a wargame of the Pacific War: the Eastern Solomons, 24 August 1942, one day long. You are game number N (replace N below with your number).

How to play. Every call to the game is one shell command, run with the Bash tool:
    PLAY N TOOL 'ARGUMENTS_JSON'
for example  PLAY N get_briefing  or  PLAY N set_leave '{"leave": "launch", "given": true}' . The answer is printed. `PLAY tools` prints every tool you have, with what it does and its arguments: read the parts you need. Use only PLAY: do not read, list or search any other file or folder, and run no other command.

Your mission is the one the briefing gives: find and sink the Japanese carriers supporting the reinforcement of Guadalcanal, and keep your own carriers in action. You see your own forces exactly and the enemy only as contacts.

You command by intent: you state what you want done and your staff orders the units. State your intents with set_intent, and give your staff leave with set_leave, including leave to launch ({"leave": "launch", "given": true}). The other commands to the staff (set_limit, hold, release, approve, withdraw_intent, set_interrupt) are yours too if you judge you need them. Do not use issue_orders before the day begins: your orders to units come from your staff. Read get_briefing first; you may read list_forces, list_contacts, list_bases, get_entity, measure, command_state, plans, staff_orders, preview and propose as you wish before you commit.
