# Account of the game (Allied, days 15 to 21)

## Aim
Joined at the start of day 15 with Lunga and Tulagi already held. Aim: keep both bases (10 + 5 points), keep Lunga's airfield open, keep it supplied and unload as many tons there as possible (0.005 a ton), hurt any enemy raid or force that came near, and keep the carriers ready for the expected carrier fight. No orders were given to units; everything went through intents.

## Intents stated and why
- hold Lunga (1) and hold Tulagi (2): the staff forms fighter cover from the air groups at the base. Lunga and Tulagi carry 15 points of base-holding.
- keep_supplied Lunga, floor 30 days, interval 10 (3), forces limit: task forces 9, 11, 3 (transports at Espiritu Santo), source base 3. Lunga was at 24 days and the convoy run lands tons, which also scores. Launch leave was given so the staff released the plan itself.
- deny waters within 4 hexes of (1,-7) (4): the staff forms search and strike_ships from Lunga's groups against ships coming from Shortland and Rabaul.
- operate_carriers for task force 1 from (4,-3) (5) and task force 2 from (5,-3) (8), with their air groups in the pool: bring both carriers within reach of Lunga so their fighters and bombers join the defence and strike what turns up.
- an interrupt at Lunga below 12 days of supply (never fired in a way that needed action).

## What I could and could not work out
Could see from the tools: supply stocks and days of supply, the staff's projections and reasons (propose, staff_orders), the contacts, the claims and the raids on Lunga. Could not see: enemy strength, carrier whereabouts (no carrier contact ever appeared), the enemy's losses and whether claims were true. I could not tell whether the 378 enemy men reported ashore at Lunga were a real threat, and without a land intent I left the Marines on defend (no unit orders allowed). One slip: the first set_limit for the second carrier intent named the wrong intent number (6, which was a cover intent the staff formed) and was refused; I redid it with intent 8.

## What happened
Daily Japanese raids of 16 to 40 aircraft hit Lunga but did little (supply and a few men lost); fighter patrols and anti-aircraft claimed about 37 aircraft. Lunga's dive bombers and Enterprise groups struck small contacts near Lunga and Tulagi. The convoy from Espiritu Santo and the cargo ships already at Lunga unloaded 4712 tons there. No carrier fight took place. Both bases stayed ours. I ran the last four days in one call (run) with the staff's standing interrupts and no further change.

Result: Allied 64.4 points, Japanese 7.8, margin 56.6, decisive victory. Breakdown: 2 destroyers sunk (2), 27 aircraft (1.35), cargo landed 23.56, Lunga open 8 of 21 days (3.8; most of the early days were before my command), Lunga held 10, Tulagi held 5, enemy men lost 18.66.
