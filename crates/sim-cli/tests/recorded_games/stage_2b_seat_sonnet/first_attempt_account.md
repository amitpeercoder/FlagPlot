# The first attempt's account (verbatim, not recorded, not verifiable)

The first game in this seat was played by a Sonnet model given the same prompt, but the server was stopped by the client before it wrote its saved game and transcript, so its calls were not kept. Its account is kept here as it was written; nothing in it can be checked against the game, and it is not the game reported. The game was then played again from the start with the calls recorded (`README.md`).

The game is over. It ended with no scored result, because this seat had no objectives of its own.

**What I was shown:** At the start, Task Force 16 (Enterprise and nine escorts) sat at (7,-5) with 27 Wildcats, 33 Dauntlesses and 14 Avengers. There were no contacts and no bases.

**What I ordered:**
- VS-5 and one of the two VT-3 Avenger groups searched the northern arc.
- VB-6 and the other Avenger group, the attack group, went on naval strike.
- VF-6 flew combat air patrol over the carrier, with the whole group kept at home.

**What happened:**
- A contact (contact:1) turned up near (7,-9). VB-6 and the Avenger attack group launched at it, 17 Dauntlesses and 7 Avengers. They attacked and claimed 8 hits on a carrier, with none sunk. Claims can be well above what really happened.
- A later strike went out at (8,-9), where the contact was no longer, and found nothing.
- Enemy bombers raided the task force. The Wildcats fired at the escorts and at the bombers, and the ships' anti-aircraft fired 123 shots. We claimed 40 enemy aircraft shot down over the force.
- One enemy aircraft attacked Enterprise. The briefing lists no ships damaged or sunk and no aircraft lost on our side.
- At the end, a large enemy force of about 14 ships (contact:2) was held at (8,-11), 223 miles away. That was outside the strike groups' 190–200 mile radius, so no further strike could go out.

I did not see the enemy's losses, and the strike reports said "none", so I cannot confirm what the strikes achieved.
