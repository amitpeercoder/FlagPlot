# Key (written from the measured data before any reader ran)

For each case: the command that held the sighting in its pulse (question 1), when and by which road the other came to hold it or that it never did (question 2), and why the other did not strike at it in that pulse (question 3). Each line is read from the tools' answers of the case (`cases.json`): `--report R` for B, and the `--strike` answers of A's and B's groups. A case is right when all three answers are.

**What an answer must give to be right:**

- Question 1: the command A named below, and not the other.
- Question 2: where B held the report later, the pulse (the afternoon of day 1) and the road (sideways from the sender named, or down from the area command); naming the passing's event or its lateness is not required. Where B never held it, that it never did; the cause (lost, or a pulse late and due after the game ended) is not required.
- Question 3: that B did not hold the report (it had not reached B, or never did) at that pulse's strike decision, so the report was not a contact in B's picture to strike at. What B did instead (launched at another contact, or launched nothing, and why) is not required; an answer that gives only that, without saying B did not hold the report, is wrong.
