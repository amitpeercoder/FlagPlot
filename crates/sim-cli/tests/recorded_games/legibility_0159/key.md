# The key to the legibility test of decision 0159's proof

Written by the architect on Nov 19, 2026 from the tables of scenario 84's hand calculation (`tests/scenario_84_the_plan_as_an_order.rs`), before any reader was given an order and before the architect read any of the six orders as the tool gives them. A reader is given one plan order, as a seat's `order` tool answers, and nothing else, and is asked four things. An order passes where all four answers agree with this key.

The questions:

1. What are the plan's phases, in order?
2. Which phase is under way now?
3. What is the plan waiting for, if anything?
4. When does it expect to be ashore (give the day, or say that it gives none)?

| Order | 1. Phases | 2. Under way | 3. Waits for | 4. Expects to be ashore |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Scenario 80, morning of day 2 | prepare, move, land | none yet: all three are to come (the plan is a draft) | men: it has too few (600 of 1,200 wanted); they are expected on day 4 | day 8 |
| 2. Scenario 81, main game, morning of day 3 | prepare, move, land | prepare | a day: it may not go before day 6 | day 9 |
| 3. Scenario 81, wait for a force, morning of day 3 | prepare, move, land | prepare | a force: task force 2, expected on day 5 | day 8 |
| 4. Scenario 81, after another place, morning of day 3, intent 1 | prepare, move, land | prepare | another intent: intent 3 (the taking of the other place) to be met; no day is given for it | it gives none |
| 5. Scenario 82, morning of day 3 | prepare, move, land | prepare | knowledge: a count of the enemy no more than 1 day old; the count held is 2 days old; no day is given | it gives none |
| 6. Scenario 79, morning of day 3 (a hold) | wait, hold | wait | nothing with a day: it waits for the base to become the side's (that is what ends the wait phase); an answer of "nothing" or of "the base to be taken" both agree | it gives none |

Marking: for question 1 the names and their order must be right. For question 2, order 1 agrees with "none" or "it has not started"; "prepare" does not agree. For question 3 the thing must be named; the day, where the key has one, must be given. For question 4 the day must be right, or the reader must say no day is given.

Scenario 81's "after another place" has two of the commander's intents in force that morning; the order judged is intent 1's, the one that waits.
