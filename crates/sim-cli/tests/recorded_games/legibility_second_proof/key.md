# Answer key: legibility test of the staff's reasons

Each reason is read alone. Question 1 passes if the answer names the place or force the reason is about (a base, a task force or an air group, as the reason names it) and the aim, the decision's purpose in the reason's terms. Question 2 passes if the answer gives at least one condition that the reason's own "Would change" or "Binding" part states. Any one condition passes. Where a row says "none stated", the reason itself lacks it; that is a finding about the reason.

## Findings about the reasons

- 14 and 16: Binding is "(none)", and the decision is a day with no order that names no group and no hex beyond (7,-5). The aim is only "operate the carrier force"; the decision itself is that fuel is not measured against a passage home. Q2 can pass only on "Would change" (a home port set). Reason 14 and 16 are near duplicates for different forces (task force 1 and task force 2).
- 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 18, 20 (no_order): the Serves line names the intent's place, but the reason decides nothing for the groups it lists; the aim is the intent's, and "no order" is part of the answer.
- 15: Evidence says stance engage and Worked sets avoid. The answer needs only the aim and a risk-level condition.
- 21 to 24: the station (3,0) is the place; "deny the waters within 0 hexes of (3,0)" is the commander's intent, not the reason's own aim.
- 4: Binding is mostly a long list of "waters to deny" hexes; the conditions that matter are the share raise, the fatigue level and the whole-circle arc.
